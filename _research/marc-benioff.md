## 2. POST-BOOK MATERIAL (2009 → 2026)

**V2MOM has not been renamed or restructured.** The five components are the same as the American Express envelope in 1999. The refinements are institutional, not conceptual:

- **Cascade formalized.** Individual → Team → Function → Corporate V2MOMs. Every one of Salesforce's ~75,000 employees today writes an annual personal V2MOM aligned to their manager's, aligned to their function's, aligned to Benioff's corporate one. Everyone can see everyone else's, searchable by employee name.
- **Publishing surface: Chatter.** Benioff's corporate V2MOM is published on Chatter (Salesforce's internal social platform). "Like dominoes," all employees then write and share theirs.
- **Cadence formalized:** annual creation at the start of each fiscal year (Salesforce fiscal year begins in February). Quarterly refresh. Mid-year formal review. Monthly / weekly reference in 1:1s.
- **Ohana + Values-first framing.** After Trailblazer (2019), the V2MOM's "Values" component became the load-bearing element in Benioff's public narrative — "values create value" as the mantra. Salesforce's official four values (Trust → Customer Success → Innovation → Equality, later joined by Sustainability) are always presented in priority order, mirroring how V2MOM asks values to be ordered.
- **Applied to acquisitions.** MuleSoft (2018, $6.5B), Tableau (2019, $15.7B), Slack (2021, $27.7B). Each integration ran a V2MOM exercise. This is not deeply documented publicly but referenced in Benioff's press appearances.
- **Ported to product / event / project scopes.** Trailhead now has an "Innovation Project V2MOM" module and Salesforce publishes an "Events V2MOM" template. V2MOM has become a general-purpose Salesforce planning primitive, not just company strategy.
- **AI-era V2MOM (FY26).** Benioff's FY26 V2MOM (published Feb 2026) centers Agentforce and "digital labor" as the year's pillar. This is the current cycle — the framework's application to the LLM/agent shift is Benioff's active work.

**The 2023 "Wellness culture" controversy** (Feb 2023) — Benioff published a corporate V2MOM whose Obstacles section read: *"Wellness culture overpowered high performance culture during pandemic. Fear of escalations for people-related issues (burnout, psychological safety, equality, etc.) can make managers reticent to performance manage their teams."* Employees called it "tone deaf" on internal Slack. Benioff removed the line within a week. Two things this reveals for the skill: (a) V2MOM at Salesforce is public inside the company — controversial phrases get scrutinized in real time; (b) the Obstacles section is not neutral bookkeeping — it names hard things and can bruise. Source: https://fortune.com/2023/02/28/salesforce-employees-wellness-culture-vs-high-performance-culture/

## 3. VOICE & TONE

**Register:** Warm, values-first, Aloha-inflected, unafraid of spiritual vocabulary (Ohana, shoshin/beginner's mind, mindfulness). NOT the register of a McKinsey memo. Benioff talks about strategy as if it were a family conversation about what matters. Read a Benioff keynote and you'll get Buddhism, philanthropy, and a sales quarter number in the same paragraph.

**Rhetorical moves**
- **"The biggest secret of our success"** — Benioff's self-description of V2MOM. Repeated across every retelling. Sets V2MOM as the alpha decision, not one tool among many.
- **Origin as parable.** He almost always starts with the American Express envelope in Hawaii, 1999. The napkin/envelope is not incidental — it's evidence that the framework works even at scale 1.
- **Contrast with Oracle.** His opening move for V2MOM is nearly always: "When I was at Oracle, I struggled with the fact that there was no written business plan or formal communication process during our growth phase." Larry Ellison's answer to "What is Oracle's five-year plan?" — there isn't one — becomes the foil.
- **Values in priority order.** Benioff insists Trust is #1, then Customer Success, then Innovation, then Equality, then Sustainability. When someone asks "aren't they all equal?" he pushes back — priority matters because *conflict* between values gets resolved by the order.
- **"Values create value."** The Trailblazer mantra. Positions Values not as culture ornament but as a strategy input that generates outputs.
- **Beginner's mind (shoshin).** Central to how he re-writes his V2MOM each year — treat the document as if writing it fresh, don't inherit last year's framing without testing it.
- **Ohana.** Hawaiian for family. Recurs whenever he explains why company-wide V2MOM transparency matters — family sees each other's work, doesn't hide it.

## 4. HEURISTICS / DO's / DON'Ts / GOTCHAS

**Order matters — and it's V→V→M→O→M, not V-M-O-M-V**
- Vision first: what are we trying to do?
- Values second: which principles govern how we do it, in priority order?
- Methods third: given the vision and values, what actions?
- Obstacles fourth: what will make the methods hard?
- Measures fifth: how will we know we've done it?
- **Why the order:** each layer constrains the next. Skipping order (e.g., jumping to Measures / KPIs first, then reverse-engineering) is the OKR trap Benioff explicitly built V2MOM to avoid.

**Values must be ordered — not a flat list**
- Salesforce's Trust → Customer Success → Innovation → Equality is deliberate. When two values conflict on a specific decision (e.g., ship faster for Innovation vs. slow down for Trust), the higher-priority value wins.
- A flat "we value X, Y, Z" list gives no decision guidance. The order is where the decision-power lives.

**Obstacles is the distinctive element — do not skip**
- Most goal-setting frameworks (OKRs, Balanced Scorecard, VMV templates) omit Obstacles. This is V2MOM's differentiator. Naming what will get in the way *before* execution surfaces risk early, and gives the team permission to talk about hard things.
- **Gotcha (2023 controversy):** Obstacles as written by the CEO are public inside the company. Language matters — naming "wellness culture" as an obstacle to "high performance culture" reads to employees as a values statement, not a neutral risk log. Frame obstacles as things to overcome, not people/behaviors to blame.

**Measures must be SMART**
- Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
- Salesforce Trailhead example: *"Achieve market share of 30% in the United States by end of fiscal year"* — SMART. *"Dominate the U.S. market!"* — not SMART.
- Two flavors accepted: **Progress Measures** (targets: revenue, share, NPS) and **Completion Measures** (binary: ship / not ship, hire / not hire).

**Length discipline**
- Vision: 1-3 sentences.
- Values: 3 (up to 5) with 1-line description each.
- Methods: 5-8 concrete actions, prioritized.
- Obstacles: 3-5, concrete.
- Measures: paired to methods, roughly one measure per method or per group of methods.
- **Total document: one page.** Benioff's original 1999 V2MOM fit on an envelope.

**Cascade rules**
- **Corporate V2MOM first.** Benioff writes his. Publishes on Chatter.
- **Function/team V2MOMs next.** Each function head writes theirs, showing how their V2MOM's methods advance the corporate methods and how their measures roll up.
- **Individual V2MOMs last.** Every employee writes their own personal V2MOM, aligned to their manager's team V2MOM.
- **Not copy-paste.** Individuals must *own* their V2MOM, not clone their manager's. Trailhead explicitly warns against this.
- **New hires get 90 days.** Salesforce policy — a new hire waits ~90 days before publishing a first V2MOM.

**Refresh cadence**
- **Annual creation** (start of fiscal year — for Salesforce, February).
- **Quarterly refresh** (updated to reflect changed context, new information).
- **Mid-year formal review** (comprehensive re-alignment with corporate).
- **Weekly / monthly in 1:1s** — managers use employee's V2MOM as the frame for check-ins.

**Common misapplications**
- **V2MOM as VMV template.** Someone reads "Vision, Values" and stops there. Result: a mission statement with three values on a poster. Not V2MOM. Methods + Obstacles + Measures are what turn it from culture ornament into planning artifact.
- **V2MOM as OKR replacement.** V2MOM subsumes OKRs (Measures = the numeric goals). But if a team uses V2MOM for context and OKRs for measurement, they're now maintaining two documents. Pick one. Salesforce picked V2MOM.
- **V2MOM as one-time offsite output.** A V2MOM that isn't referenced weekly in 1:1s and updated quarterly is a dead document. "If it's not on the V2MOM it won't get done" — Trailhead.
- **Individual V2MOMs as clones of manager's.** Kills the point. Individual V2MOMs must reflect individual choices about how *this person* advances the team's methods.
- **Obstacles as CYA / political theatre.** Naming obstacles you're not going to address is throat-clearing. Every obstacle needs at least one method aimed at overcoming it.
- **Measures decoupled from Methods.** A measure without a method is a wish. A method without a measure is a task list. They must pair.

**Do's**
- **Do write it yourself.** Even Benioff writes his V2MOM personally each year. Delegating to a chief of staff removes the CEO's real thinking from the document.
- **Do publish it internally.** Transparency is what makes cascade work.
- **Do treat it as a living document.** Change it when context changes.
- **Do use it in 1:1s.** The V2MOM is the frame for the manager-employee conversation.

**Don'ts**
- **Don't skip Obstacles.** It's the distinctive element.
- **Don't leave Values unordered.** The order is the decision guidance.
- **Don't write more than one page.** Discipline forces prioritization.
- **Don't inherit last year's V2MOM unchanged.** Beginner's mind — write fresh.

## 5. REAL CASES BENIOFF USES PUBLICLY

**The 1999 original Salesforce V2MOM** — written by Benioff on a large American Express envelope in the company's first weeks. Preserved and framed by co-founder Parker Harris; presented to Benioff on IPO day (2004). The public version:
- **Vision:** Rapidly create a world-class Internet company / site for sales-force automation.
- **Values:** World-class organization; time to market; functional; usability; value to customer/investor.
- **Methods:** Hire the team; finalize product spec + technical architecture; rapidly develop to beta and production; build partnerships with e-commerce / content / hosting companies; build launch plan; develop exit strategy (IPO/acquisition).
- **Obstacles:** Developer recruitment; product manager / business development hiring.
- **Measures:** Prototype is state-of-the-art; high-quality functional system; partnerships online and integrated; regarded as leader and visionary; "We are all rich."

**Every annual Salesforce V2MOM since 1999** — Benioff has written and published one per fiscal year. Not all are public. The FY24 V2MOM (Feb 2023) became public via the Wellness Culture controversy. The FY26 V2MOM (Dec 2025 preview, Feb 2026 publish) was previewed to Business Insider — Agentforce as central pillar.

**Buddy Media's post-acquisition adoption** — Kass and Mike Lazerow, after selling Buddy Media to Salesforce for $745M in 2012, took V2MOM to their venture portfolio of 100+ startups. Cited in Forbes 2025 as evidence the framework scales down as well as up.

**Salesforce Events V2MOM** — a public Salesforce PDF (2022) applies V2MOM to a specific event ("Leverage V2MOM for Your Events"). Shows the framework's ability to scope down from company-wide to a single project.

**Innovation Project V2MOM** (Trailhead module) — Salesforce's teaching that V2MOM works at project scope, not just annual company scope.

## 6. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER FRAMEWORKS

**vs. OKRs (Doerr / Grove / Google)**
- OKRs = Objective + 3-5 Key Results. Two components.
- V2MOM = Vision + Values + Methods + Obstacles + Measures. Five.
- Overlap: V2MOM's "Vision + one Method + its Measure" ≈ one OKR. The rest of V2MOM (Values, Obstacles) is what OKRs omit.
- Benioff's implicit critique: OKRs give you *what* to measure without *why it matters* (Vision/Values) or *what will get in the way* (Obstacles). OKRs are execution accountability; V2MOM is strategic alignment.
- Practical guide: V2MOM subsumes OKRs. If you're using both, your V2MOM's Measures section *is* your OKRs, plus you also carry Values + Obstacles as first-class context. Don't run parallel systems.

**vs. VMV (Vision, Mission, Values) templates**
- VMV is a static culture artifact — hangs on a wall.
- V2MOM is a dynamic planning artifact — updated quarterly, referenced weekly.
- The V and Values of V2MOM look like VMV but the Methods/Obstacles/Measures below make it a plan, not a poster.

**vs. Balanced Scorecard (Kaplan & Norton)**
- Balanced Scorecard organizes measures across four perspectives (Financial, Customer, Internal Process, Learning & Growth).
- V2MOM doesn't segment measures by perspective; it groups them under the methods they measure.
- Balanced Scorecard is a measurement architecture. V2MOM is an alignment architecture that includes measurement.

**vs. Playing to Win cascade (Martin & Lafley)**
- P2W: 5 questions about competitive strategy (aspiration, where-to-play, how-to-win, capabilities, management systems).
- V2MOM: 5 elements about organizational alignment (vision, values, methods, obstacles, measures).
- Different objects: P2W is a competitive-strategy tool ("how do we win against competitors?"); V2MOM is a cross-organization alignment tool ("how do we all pull in the same direction?").
- Complementary: use P2W to *make* strategic choices about how to win; use V2MOM to *cascade and align* the resulting plan across the organization.

**vs. Rumelt's kernel of strategy (diagnosis + guiding policy + coherent action)**
- Rumelt is diagnostic-first: what is the crux of the challenge? What guiding policy addresses it?
- V2MOM is aspiration-first: what do we want, and what will it take?
- If you don't yet know what the pivotal problem is, Rumelt's kernel goes first. Once you know what you're trying to do, V2MOM aligns the org around doing it.

**vs. MBOs (Drucker's Management by Objectives)**
- MBO ancestry — V2MOM is arguably an offspring of MBO with values + obstacles added.
- MBO focused on individual objectives negotiated with manager. V2MOM adds vertical alignment (cascade), values-first framing, and obstacle-naming.

**vs. Amazon's Working Backwards / PR-FAQ**
- Working Backwards is a product-decision tool (write the launch press release before building).
- V2MOM is an organization-planning tool.
- They pair: use PR-FAQ to decide *what* to build; use V2MOM to align the org on *building it*.
