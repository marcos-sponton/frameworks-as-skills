## Books (the SVPG canonical stack)

- **INSPIRED: How to Create Tech Products Customers Love** (Wiley, 2008; 2nd ed. 2018) — Marty Cagan. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/INSPIRED-Create-Tech-Products-Customers/dp/1119387507) · [SVPG book page](https://www.svpg.com/books/inspired-how-to-create-tech-products-customers-love-2nd-edition/). Foundational — Product Team, four big risks, product discovery, PM role definition.
- **EMPOWERED: Ordinary People, Extraordinary Products** (Wiley, 2020) — Marty Cagan with Chris Jones. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/EMPOWERED-Ordinary-Extraordinary-Products-Silicon/dp/111969129X) · [SVPG book page](https://www.svpg.com/books/empowered-ordinary-people-extraordinary-products/). Product leadership, coaching, staffing, vision + strategy, missionaries vs. mercenaries.
- **TRANSFORMED: Moving to the Product Operating Model** (Wiley, 2024) — Marty Cagan with Lea Hickman, Chris Jones, Christian Idiodi, John Moore. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Transformed-Becoming-Product-Driven-Company-Silicon/dp/1119697336) · [SVPG book page](https://www.svpg.com/books/transformed-moving-to-the-product-operating-model/). The transformation playbook, 20 First Principles (Ch. 15–19), three first-person transformation case studies.
- **LOVED: How to Rethink Marketing for Tech Products** (Wiley, 2022) — Martina Lauchengco with Marty Cagan foreword. [SVPG book page](https://www.svpg.com/books/loved-how-to-rethink-marketing-for-tech-products/). The SVPG product marketing companion.
- **Product Is Hard SVPG Box Set** (Wiley, 2024) — all four bundled. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Product-Hard-SVPG-Box-Set/dp/1394326262).
