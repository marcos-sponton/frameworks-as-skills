# Challenger + JOLT — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts

- **The Challenger Sale: Taking Control of the Customer Conversation** — Matthew Dixon & Brent Adamson. Portfolio/Penguin, 2011. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Challenger-Sale-Control-Customer-Conversation/dp/1591844355). The canonical source for the 5 profiles + 3 T's + Commercial Insight.
- **The Challenger Customer: Selling to the Hidden Influencer Who Can Multiply Your Results** — Brent Adamson, Matthew Dixon, Pat Spenner, Nick Toman. Portfolio/Penguin, 2015. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Challenger-Customer-Selling-Influencer-Multiply/dp/1591848156). The 6.8-stakeholder / Mobilizer-Talker-Blocker work.
- **The JOLT Effect: How High Performers Overcome Customer Indecision** — Matthew Dixon & Ted McKenna. Portfolio/Penguin, 2022. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/JOLT-Effect-Performers-Overcome-Indecision/dp/0593538102). Companion site: [jolteffect.com](https://www.jolteffect.com/). The 40-60% no-decision research + J-O-L-T framework.

## Other Dixon books

- **The Effortless Experience: Conquering the New Battleground for Customer Loyalty** — Matthew Dixon, Nick Toman, Rick DeLisi. Portfolio/Penguin, 2013. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Effortless-Experience-Conquering-Battleground-Customer/dp/1591845815). Adjacent (customer service).
- **The Activator Advantage: What Today's Rainmakers Do Differently** — Matthew Dixon, Ted McKenna, Rory Channer, Karen Freeman. Harvard Business Review Press, 2025. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Activator-Advantage-Todays-Rainmakers-Differently/dp/B0D8V4SFGP). The doer-seller / rainmaker research in professional services.
