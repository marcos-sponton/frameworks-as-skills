# Matt Dixon's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Matt Dixon publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and by topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay/talk/podcast that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Dixon's research is not frozen. The JOLT research came 11 years after Challenger; the Activator research came 3 years after JOLT. Skipping his live sources = the skill drifts out of date. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (deal at risk of no-decision, Mobilizer identification, rep coaching, professional-services BD), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/watch/listen, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

## Primary channel — HBR

**Archive:** [Matt Dixon on HBR](https://hbr.org/search?term=matthew+dixon) (20+ articles across 15+ years)

### Most cited pieces

- [**What Today's Rainmakers Do Differently** (Nov-Dec 2023)](https://hbr.org/2023/11/what-todays-rainmakers-do-differently) — Activator research, in HBR 10 Must Reads for 2025.
- [**Stop Losing Sales to Customer Indecision** (June 2022)](https://hbr.org/2022/06/stop-losing-sales-to-customer-indecision) — the JOLT companion article.
- **Reinventing Customer Service** (Nov-Dec 2018)
- **Kick-Ass Customer Service** (Jan-Feb 2017)
- **Dismantling the Sales Machine** (Nov 2013)
- **The End of Solution Sales** (Jul-Aug 2012) — the Challenger HBR-companion article; one of the most-read sales pieces of the decade
- **Stop Trying to Delight Your Customers** (Jul-Aug 2010) — the Effortless Experience companion; still cited when the topic is loyalty vs. delight

### Note on HBR

Dixon's HBR articles are often the tightest form of his arguments (2000-3000 words each). When the user wants the argument without buying the book, point them to HBR.
