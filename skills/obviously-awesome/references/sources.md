# Obviously Awesome — Sources

## Primary texts

- **Obviously Awesome: How to Nail Product Positioning So Customers Get It, Buy It, Love It** — April Dunford, Ambient Press, 2019 (expanded 2nd edition, 2026). [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Obviously-Awesome-Product-Positioning-Customers/dp/1999023005) · [Author's page](https://www.aprildunford.com/books). The canonical source. Read it.
- **Sales Pitch: How to Craft a Story to Stand Out and Win** — April Dunford, Ambient Press, 2023. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Sales-Pitch-Craft-Story-Stand/dp/1999023021). The operational extension of positioning into the B2B sales conversation.
