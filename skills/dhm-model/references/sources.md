# DHM Model — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary text — the Medium essay series (~2018-2020)

Gibson Biddle has not written a book. The canonical text is the 12-part essay series "How to Define Your Product Strategy" on Medium:

- **Intro: "How to Define Your Product Strategy"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/intro-to-product-strategy-60bdf72b17e3](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/intro-to-product-strategy-60bdf72b17e3). The anchor essay. Frames product strategy as hypotheses, introduces the DHM filter.
- **#1 "The DHM Model"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/2-the-dhm-model-6ea5dfd80792](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/2-the-dhm-model-6ea5dfd80792). Defines Delight, Hard-to-copy, Margin-enhancing. Netflix cancel rate case.
- **#2 "From DHM to Product Strategy"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/2-from-dhm-to-product-strategy-a3781b2aadca](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/2-from-dhm-to-product-strategy-a3781b2aadca). The four types of hard-to-copy advantage. Netflix 2005 strategies.
- **#3 "The Strategy/Metric/Tactic Lock-up"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/3-the-strategy-metric-tactic-lock-up-b7539ec69a7e](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/3-the-strategy-metric-tactic-lock-up-b7539ec69a7e). Connecting strategy to metrics to projects. Netflix pods.
- **#4 "Proxy Metrics"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/4-proxy-metrics-a82dd30ca810](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/4-proxy-metrics-a82dd30ca810). High-level vs. proxy metrics. Netflix queue depth, ratings, engagement proxies.
- **#5 "Working Bottom-up"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/5-working-bottom-up-426447b2b876](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/5-working-bottom-up-426447b2b876). Scan project list to identify implicit strategies.
- **#6 "A Strategy for Each Swimlane"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/6-a-strategy-for-each-swimlane-38be75f65129](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/6-a-strategy-for-each-swimlane-38be75f65129). Product areas as swimlanes with own strategies and metrics.
- **#7 "The Product Roadmap"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/7-the-product-roadmap-bf8830d81c19](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/7-the-product-roadmap-bf8830d81c19). Roadmap as expression of strategy.
- **#8 "The GLEe Model"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/6-the-glee-model-6af740bdf3b1](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/6-the-glee-model-6af740bdf3b1). Get Big, Lead, Expand. Netflix long-term phasing.
- **#9 "The GEM Model"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/9-the-gem-model-65c89face5de](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/9-the-gem-model-65c89face5de). Growth, Engagement, Monetization prioritization.
- **#10 "How to Run A Quarterly Product Strategy Meeting"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/10-the-quarterly-product-strategy-meeting-b5c0c1b12722](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/10-the-quarterly-product-strategy-meeting-b5c0c1b12722). Operating cadence for strategy.
- **#11 "A Case Study: Netflix 2020"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/netflix-today-64fe91e2c693](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/netflix-today-64fe91e2c693). All frameworks applied to modern Netflix.
- **#12 "A Startup Case Study: Chegg"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/11-case-study-chegg-8044679939f9](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/11-case-study-chegg-8044679939f9). DHM and GLEe applied to Chegg.
- **#13 (TLDR) "Summary of All the Product Strategy Frameworks"** — [https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/12-step-by-step-exercises-to-define-your-product-strategy-b27a81edc918](https://gibsonbiddle.medium.com/12-step-by-step-exercises-to-define-your-product-strategy-b27a81edc918). Step-by-step exercises across all frameworks.
