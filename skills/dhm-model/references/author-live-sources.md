> Where Gibson Biddle publishes, teaches, and speaks. Use this index to find the right source for a specific situation.

## Gibson Biddle — bio

Former VP of Product at Netflix (2005-2010), where he helped the company grow from ~1M to 20M members and navigate the transition from DVDs-by-mail to streaming. Chief Product Officer at Chegg (2010-2015), where the company pivoted from textbook rental to digital homework help and went public. Since leaving Chegg, Biddle has spent a decade as a speaker, teacher, and writer on product strategy and product leadership. Teaches at Stanford and INSEAD. Lives between teaching cohorts and thru-hiking.
