# V2MOM — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary text — Marc Benioff's books

- **Behind the Cloud: The Untold Story of How Salesforce.com Went from Idea to Billion-Dollar Company** — Marc Benioff & Carlye Adler. Jossey-Bass, 2009. The primary source for V2MOM. Includes the American Express envelope origin story, the five-element walkthrough, and the "biggest secret of our success" claim. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Behind-Cloud-Salesforce-com-Billion-Dollar-Company/dp/B08XLGFP8V) · [Archive.org](https://archive.org/details/behindclouduntol00beni).
- **Trailblazer: The Power of Business as the Greatest Platform for Change** — Marc Benioff & Monica Langley. Currency, 2019. The Ohana / values-first re-framing. Introduces "values create value" as mantra and defends Trust as #1 value. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Trailblazer-Business-Greatest-Platform-Change/dp/1984825194) · [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Trailblazer.html?id=c3uODwAAQBAJ).

## Earlier / adjacent Benioff books

- **The Business of Changing the World** (2008) — establishes the 1-1-1 philanthropy model.
- **Compassionate Capitalism** (2004) — early values-first framing.

Neither is V2MOM-primary; both help establish the register.
