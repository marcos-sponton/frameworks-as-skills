# V2MOM — Material posterior a los libros / Evolution 1999 → 2026

> **The differential of this skill.** *Behind the Cloud* (2009) laid down the V2MOM story and mechanics; *Trailblazer* (2019) added the values-first / Ohana framing. Since then, V2MOM has evolved not conceptually — the five elements have not changed — but institutionally: from a 4-person envelope to a 75,000-employee cascade published on Chatter, applied to project scopes, and now oriented around AI/Agentforce.
>
> This file captures what the books don't: the mechanics of cascade at scale, the annual press cycle around each fiscal year's V2MOM, the 2023 controversy, the FY26 AI pivot, and the third-party adoption pattern (Buddy Media / Lazerow portfolio).

## The framework itself has not changed

The five elements are the same as the American Express envelope in 1999:

- Vision
- Values
- Methods
- Obstacles
- Measures

Same order. Same one-page constraint. Same "living document" framing.

**Refinements are all institutional, not conceptual.** If someone tells you V2MOM has been renamed or restructured, they're likely conflating it with an internal Salesforce process variant, not a change to V2MOM itself.

## Cascade formalized (2009 → 2019)

In 1999, "cascade" meant Benioff wrote it and emailed his seven employees. By the time of *Trailblazer* (2019), the mechanics were:

1. **Corporate V2MOM.** Benioff writes it personally each fiscal year. Publishes on **Chatter** (Salesforce's internal social platform).
2. **Function V2MOMs.** Each function head (Sales, Product, Engineering, People, Marketing, Legal, etc.) writes theirs within days of Benioff's publication.
3. **Team V2MOMs.** Each team within a function.
4. **Individual V2MOMs.** Every employee — currently ~75,000 — writes their own personal V2MOM aligned to their team's.

**Publishing surface: Chatter.** Every V2MOM is searchable. Any employee can pull up any other employee's V2MOM on their phone. This transparency is what makes cascade a mechanism, not an aspiration.

**"Like dominoes."** Benioff's framing for how the cascade sequences — corporate first, then functions in the days after, then teams, then individuals over the following weeks. Not synchronized; propagating.

## Cadence formalized

- **Annual creation.** Start of Salesforce fiscal year (February). Benioff typically previews the corporate V2MOM in December/January press.
- **Quarterly refresh.** Teams update their V2MOMs to reflect changed context.
- **Mid-year formal review.** Comprehensive re-alignment with corporate.
- **Weekly / monthly reference.** 1:1s frame around the employee's V2MOM.

Trailhead's operating principle: *"If it's not on the V2MOM it won't get done."*

## Ohana + Values-first framing (Trailblazer, 2019)

*Trailblazer* wasn't a revision of V2MOM; it was a re-framing of what makes V2MOM work at scale. The load-bearing shift: **Values are not decorative culture; they are strategy inputs.**

The mantra: **"Values create value."**

Salesforce's Ohana values, in priority order:

1. **Trust**
2. **Customer Success**
3. **Innovation**
4. **Equality**
5. **Sustainability** (added post-Trailblazer)

**Ohana** — Hawaiian for family. Benioff spent time in Hawaii after his first tech success and imported the concept as the anchor for how Salesforce operates. In V2MOM terms: Ohana means every employee's V2MOM is visible to every other employee. Family sees each other's work.

**Beginner's mind (shoshin).** From *Trailblazer* and reinforced in Benioff's 2024 Fortune Leadership Next interview: rewrite each year's V2MOM as if writing it fresh, don't inherit last year's framing unchanged.

**Mindfulness zones.** Salesforce offices have designated mindfulness zones where employees put phones away and clear their heads. Not directly a V2MOM feature, but part of the surrounding practice — the state of mind for writing a good V2MOM is not reactive-inbox mode.

## Applied to acquisitions (2018–2021)

Salesforce made three defining acquisitions in this period:

- **MuleSoft** — $6.5B, 2018 (integration).
- **Tableau** — $15.7B, 2019 (analytics).
- **Slack** — $27.7B, 2021 (collaboration).

Each integration ran a V2MOM exercise to align the acquired org with Salesforce's plan. Not deeply documented publicly, but referenced in Benioff's press appearances and confirmed by Marc & Kass Lazerow's post-Buddy Media testimony that V2MOM is used systematically in Salesforce M&A.

**The Buddy Media / Lazerow example.** Kass and Mike Lazerow sold Buddy Media to Salesforce for $745M in 2012 and adopted V2MOM at Buddy Media. After leaving Salesforce to become venture investors, they took V2MOM into their portfolio of 100+ startups, generating over $10B in aggregate value. Their line, per Forbes (2025): V2MOM is *"just as powerful for a one-person startup as it is for a 70,000-person organization."*

## Applied to smaller scopes (Trailhead + Salesforce Events)

V2MOM at Salesforce is no longer only annual/company. Two published examples of scope-down applications:

- **Innovation Project V2MOM.** Trailhead module teaching V2MOM at project scope. Same five elements, tightened horizon.
- **Events V2MOM.** [Salesforce PDF (2022)](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2022/06/LeverageV2MOM.pdf) applying V2MOM to a single event (product launch, conference, campaign).

**Implication for practitioners:** don't assume V2MOM is only for annual company planning. It's a general-purpose Salesforce planning primitive that scales down to a project or up to a company.

## The 2023 Wellness Culture controversy

**February 13, 2023.** Benioff published Salesforce's FY24 corporate V2MOM on Chatter. The Obstacles section included:

> *"Wellness culture overpowered high performance culture during pandemic. Fear of escalations for people-related issues (burnout, psychological safety, equality, etc.) can make managers reticent to performance manage their teams."*

Salesforce employees responded on internal Slack calling it *"most disturbing and tone deaf"* and *"a sad excuse."* The Wall Street Journal picked it up. Fortune covered it a week later.

**February 20, 2023.** Benioff revised the V2MOM and removed the line entirely.

**Two lessons for the skill:**

1. **V2MOM is a public artifact inside the company.** Every word gets scrutinized in real time. That transparency is the mechanism that makes cascade real — but it also means the CEO's obstacle-naming has values-statement weight, whether or not that's intended.
2. **Obstacles as a section is easy to get wrong.** Naming an obstacle correctly is a leadership move (surfacing a hard truth the team can address together). Naming it wrong is a values-statement in obstacle-clothing (blaming a group of employees for the CEO's productivity concerns). The 2023 incident is the case study to point at when a team asks *"how do I frame an obstacle without it landing badly?"*

Fortune's coverage: https://fortune.com/2023/02/28/salesforce-employees-wellness-culture-vs-high-performance-culture/

## FY26 — the AI / Agentforce V2MOM

**December 2025.** Benioff previewed his FY26 (fiscal year 2026, February 2026 – January 2027) V2MOM to Business Insider before publishing internally. The central pillar: **Agentforce and "digital labor."** V2MOM's Methods section was reorganized around Salesforce's agentic-AI product line; Measures shifted from traditional SaaS metrics toward AI-adoption and agent-deployment measures.

This is Benioff applying V2MOM to a paradigm shift — the LLM/agent era in enterprise software. The framework absorbs the shift without changing structure: same five elements, radically different Methods and Measures.

**Reference:** Business Insider interview (Dec 2025) — https://dnyuz.com/2025/12/04/marc-benioff-is-writing-salesforces-annual-strategy-document-he-shared-the-key-details-early-with-business-insider/

**Implication for practitioners:** when your industry shifts, don't abandon V2MOM. Rewrite Methods and Measures around the shift. The Vision and Values may stay largely the same; the plan changes.

## Third-party adoption

V2MOM has quietly spread beyond Salesforce, mostly via people who worked at or sold to Salesforce and took it with them:

- **Buddy Media / Kass & Mike Lazerow** — 100+ portfolio startups.
- **Salesforce alumni** — many former Salesforce execs continue using V2MOM at their next companies. Not systematically tracked, but referenced in interviews across the ecosystem.
- **Template proliferation.** Every major planning-tool vendor (Lattice, ClickUp, Craft, Cascade.app, Notion) now offers a V2MOM template. Distribution mostly organic; Salesforce doesn't push it.

**Salesforce's own posture** — Salesforce publishes V2MOM openly (blog, Trailhead) rather than treating it as proprietary. Benioff's stance: the framework travels well precisely because it's simple, and Salesforce benefits from more people thinking in it.

## What's likely to change next

The five elements are unlikely to change — Benioff has now used them for 27 years without altering them. What's likely to evolve:

- **AI-authored V2MOM drafts.** Salesforce Agentforce is being integrated into internal workflows; expect V2MOMs increasingly drafted with AI assistance and refined by humans. Benioff has already noted in the Dec 2025 BI interview that he used AI tools "liberally" in answering questions about his FY26 V2MOM.
- **Real-time V2MOM.** As Chatter/Slack integration deepens, V2MOMs may shift from quarterly-refresh to continuous-refresh with AI-generated change alerts.
- **Cross-org V2MOM** for AI agents. If Agentforce agents become part of an org's "workforce," they may need V2MOMs too — aligned to their team's, cascading from the corporate one. This is speculative but consistent with Benioff's framing of Agentforce as "digital labor."

None of this changes the framework. All of it changes how the framework is authored and maintained.
