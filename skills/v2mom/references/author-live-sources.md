# V2MOM — Live sources for Marc Benioff and Salesforce

> Where Benioff and Salesforce publish about V2MOM today. Use this index to freshen the skill each year — the annual V2MOM press cycle (December → February) is the reliable anchor for updates.

## Bottom line

Unlike Roger Martin (weekly Medium essays) or Richard Rumelt (semi-regular HBR + interviews), **Benioff is not a live essayist on V2MOM**. He wrote about it definitively in 2009 (*Behind the Cloud*) and re-framed it via Ohana/values in 2019 (*Trailblazer*), and then relies on:

1. The **annual Salesforce V2MOM press cycle** (Dec–Feb each year).
2. Salesforce's **evergreen properties** — Trailhead modules, blog posts, PDFs — that don't change often but are the official curriculum.
3. His **social presence** on X and LinkedIn.
4. **Keynote appearances** — Dreamforce annually plus press interviews (Fortune, CNBC, Business Insider, Bloomberg, All-In, Masters of Scale).

Freshness comes from the annual cycle, not from a weekly stream. Plan the skill's refresh around that.

## Refresh cadence for this skill

- **Every February.** Check for Benioff's newly-published fiscal-year V2MOM commentary. Update `post-book.md` and `examples.md` with the year's pillar.
- **Every September.** Watch Dreamforce keynote for V2MOM framing shifts. Update `voice-and-tone.md` if new vocabulary surfaces.
- **When Salesforce updates Trailhead.** Salesforce occasionally revises the V2MOM Trailhead module — worth checking `method.md` against the current unit content annually.
- **Ad hoc.** If Benioff publishes a new book (unlikely in the next 2-3 years but possible), or another public V2MOM controversy surfaces, update `heuristics.md`.
