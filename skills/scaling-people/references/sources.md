## Attributed collaborators / sources Claire cites

Claire is unusually generous in citing her sources. The skill mirrors that — when a move originates elsewhere, we name the origin.

- **Fred Kofman — Conscious Business.** Source for victim/player and left-hand column detoxification. Claire cites in the book and multiple podcasts.
- **Marty Linsky** — the *"leadership is about driving change, management is about creating stability"* framing. Claire quotes.
- **Andy Grove — High Output Management.** The load-bearing 1:1 tradition Claire builds on. Cross-linked to [[high-output-management]] skill.
- **Kim Scott — Radical Candor.** The feedback tradition Claire operates in. Cross-linked to [[radical-candor]] skill.
- **Jeff Bezos / Amazon** — memo culture. Cross-linked to [[working-backwards]] skill.
- **Gokul Rajaram — SPADE framework.** Compatible decision tool Claire endorses.
- **Ben Horowitz — The Hard Thing About Hard Things.** *"The struggle."* Claire extends.
- **Patrick + John Collison — Stripe founders.** The model of exec-hiring done well; Claire's own hire.
- **Reid Hoffman.** The "wouldn't hire you to manage a McDonald's" story on active feedback culture.
- **Elad Gil — High Growth Handbook.** Claire was interviewed for it and cites it.
