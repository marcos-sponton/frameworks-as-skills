## Research cited by Knapp

- **Jakob Nielsen — "Why You Only Need to Test with 5 Users"** (2000). Nielsen Norman Group. The research basis for Sprint's "5 customers on Friday" rule.
- **Charlan Nemeth — "In Defense of Troublemakers"** (2018). The research Knapp cites to support individual work over group brainstorming.
