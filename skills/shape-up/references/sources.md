## Attribution note on quotes

Where verbatim quotes appear in this skill's reference files, they are attributed with:
- **Source** (book chapter, essay URL, podcast + episode).
- **Year** (2019 book vs. 2022+ post-book material — the distinction matters).

Do not collapse the 2019 canon and the 2022+ evolution into a single flat voice. Framing (2022), the "Pitch → Package" rename, and the 2025 *Common Pitfalls* piece are all Singer's current teaching, but they are *evolutions* of the 2019 book, not restatements of it.
