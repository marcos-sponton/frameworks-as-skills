## Notes on gaps and open ground

- **Grove's HBR archive is thin as author.** Grove wrote comparatively few HBR pieces under his own byline. Most durable HBR content *about* Grove appeared post-2016 as tributes (Burgelman, Christensen).
- **Stanford GSB case archive is not publicly indexed.** If a future contributor has Stanford access, adding case-title metadata to `author-live-sources.md` would strengthen the skill.
- **Grove-on-AI is thin.** Grove died before the modern AI wave. Third parties apply his SIP + 10X framework to AI competitive dynamics, but these are applied rather than primary sources.
- **International adoption of Grove's method is under-documented.** *HOM* has been widely translated and adopted; non-US case studies are open ground for community PRs.
