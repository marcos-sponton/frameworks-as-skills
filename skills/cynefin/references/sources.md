# Cynefin — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary text — the HBR paper

- **A Leader's Framework for Decision Making** — David J. Snowden & Mary E. Boone. *Harvard Business Review*, November 2007. [https://hbr.org/2007/11/a-leaders-framework-for-decision-making](https://hbr.org/2007/11/a-leaders-framework-for-decision-making). Winner of the Academy of Management outstanding practitioner-oriented publication. The widest-read introduction to Cynefin. Read it.

## Foundational academic paper

- **The new dynamics of strategy: Sense-making in a complex and complicated world** — Cynthia F. Kurtz & David J. Snowden. *IBM Systems Journal* 42(3), 2003. The original paper introducing what would become Cynefin. Domains: Known / Knowable / Complex / Chaotic.

## Canonical technical reference (wiki)

- **Cynefin.io wiki** — [https://cynefin.io](https://cynefin.io). Community-maintained. Current on all technical points where the HBR paper is dated.
  - [Cynefin Domains](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Cynefin_Domains) — the five domains, the cliff, liminality.
  - [Aporetic Turn](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Aporetic_Turn) — the 2019–20 split into Aporetic and Confused.
  - [Constraints](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Constraints) — three types (rigid / governing / enabling); endoskeleton metaphor.
  - [Estuarine framework](https://cynefin.io/wiki/Estuarine_framework) — six sub-types of constraint, two borders, 7-step process.

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **Snowden's individual pre-2020 blog posts** — thecynefin.co has thousands of archives; only recent posts (2024–26) are systematically indexed here.
- **Full transcripts of YouTube talks** — only titles indexed.
- **Original IBM-era publications** — the 2003 IBM Systems Journal paper is cited but not deeply worked through.
- **EU Field Guide full text** — the methodology is referenced but the Field Guide itself has not been indexed in depth.
- **Estuarine Mapping deeper case examples** — the framework is newer and cases are less-documented than for Cynefin proper.
- **Practitioner applications outside Snowden's own writing** — Cynefin has been applied in health, safety, education, defense, public policy. Individual practitioner writeups (Chris Corrigan, Sonja Blignaut, Alexandra van der Hilst, Neil Perkin) are only lightly indexed.
- **Snowden's academic publications** beyond the 2003 IBM Systems Journal paper and the 2007 HBR paper.
- **Direct engagement with peer complexity thinkers** (Jean Boulton, Ralph Stacey, Alicia Juarrero) — mentioned in passing but not compared.
