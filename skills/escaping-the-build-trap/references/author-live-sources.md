# Escaping the Build Trap — Melissa Perri's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Melissa Perri publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and by topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific episode/essay/post that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Perri publishes weekly (podcast) and often (Substack, LinkedIn). The build trap frame is not frozen in 2018. Skipping her live sources = the skill drifts out of date. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current — especially for the *Product Thinking* podcast, which drops a new episode roughly every Wednesday.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (roadmap fight, OKRs going sideways, hiring a CPO, standing up Product Ops, dealing with Founder Mode), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/listen, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

## Fuente principal (long-form) — melissaperri.com/blog

**Live URL:** [https://melissaperri.com/blog](https://melissaperri.com/blog)

- Cadence: 1–4 posts/year since 2018. Low cadence, high signal.
- Where her most durable frameworks first appear.

### Key posts (chronological — shows the evolution of her thinking)

- [**Rethinking the Product Roadmap**](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2014/05/19/rethinking-the-product-roadmap) · May 2014. Origin of the **Problem Roadmap**. Foundational.
- [**Product Operations: The Fuel for Winning Product Strategies**](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2019/7/19/product-operations-the-fuel-for-winning-product-strategies) · Jul 2019. First public definition of Product Ops. Later expanded into the 2023 book.
- [**Prioritization Shouldn't Be Hard**](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2019/10/31/prioritization) · Oct 2019. Key insight: prioritization is only hard when strategy is missing.
- [**Are We Getting Rid of Product Managers?**](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2023/7/7/are-we-getting-rid-of-product-managers) · Jul 2023. Response to Brian Chesky/Airbnb. Predates the Founder Mode essay.
- [**Building a Great Product Management Organization**](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2024/7/16/building-a-great-product-management-organization) · Jul 2024. Introduces the **Four Dimensions** framework — her post-book audit model.
- [**How to Get Clarity When Your Company's Strategy is 'Fuzzy'**](https://melissaperri.com/blog/2024/10/22/how-to-get-clarity-when-your-companys-strategy-is-fuzzy) · Oct 2024. The Fuzzy Strategy diagnostic + 4 value drivers.
