## Adjacent authors referenced in this skill

Perri's frame composes with several adjacent frameworks. Sources for each:

- **Marty Cagan / SVPG** — *Inspired* (2008/2018), *Empowered* (2020), *Transformed* (2024). [svpg.com](https://www.svpg.com/). Cagan owns the "Product Operating Model" phrase at the top-level org altitude; Perri operates one level down. Cagan explicitly credits Perri's Product Ops definition.
- **Teresa Torres** — *Continuous Discovery Habits* (2021). [producttalk.org](https://www.producttalk.org/). Torres's Opportunity Solution Tree fits inside Perri's Kata.
- **Ryan Singer / Basecamp** — *Shape Up*. [basecamp.com/shapeup](https://basecamp.com/shapeup). Compatible on delivery cadence; Perri's frame is broader.
- **Bob Moesta / Jim Kalbach — Jobs to be Done** — Perri stays in "customer problems" language; JTBD fits inside her Problem Roadmap as a discovery method.
- **Eric Ries — Lean Startup** — foundational influence. Perri keeps "build-measure-learn" and explicitly redefines MVP as "minimum effort to learn."
- **Joshua Arnold — 4 value drivers** (Increase Revenue / Protect Revenue / Reduce Costs / Avoid Costs) — used by Perri in the Fuzzy Strategy diagnostic. [strategicblueprint.co](https://www.strategicblueprint.co/) / see Perri's Oct 2024 blog post.
- **Mike Rother — Toyota Kata** — origin of the Product Kata pattern Perri adapted.
- **Google — HEART framework** — Happiness, Engagement, Adoption, Retention, Task success. Recommended by Perri.
- **Dave McClure — Pirate Metrics / AARRR** — Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue. Recommended by Perri.
