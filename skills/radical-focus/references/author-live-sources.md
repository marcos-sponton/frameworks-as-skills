# Radical Focus — Christina Wodtke's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Christina Wodtke publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and by topic. Designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay / podcast / talk that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Wodtke's method has evolved substantially since the 2nd edition of *Radical Focus* (2021). Skipping her live sources = the skill drifts out of date. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (a Monday commitments meeting to run, a health metric to design, a cascading-vs-alignment debate, KPI vs OKR confusion), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/watch it, or (b) WebFetch it inline for depth.

### Essays clave organizadas por tema

#### The OKR structure — how to write one

- [The Art of the OKR](https://eleganthack.com/the-art-of-the-okr/) — canonical: what makes a good Objective + Key Results, the 5/10 confidence rule.

#### Health Metrics — the anti-gaming guard

- [What Sam Altman Just Taught Us About OKRs (Without Meaning To)](https://eleganthack.com/what-sam-altman-just-taught-us-about-okrs-without-meaning-to/) (Dec 2025) — the OpenAI "code red" case. The clearest single essay on Health Metrics.

#### One Objective at a time — the "radical" in Radical Focus

- [One Objective to Rule Them All](https://cwodtke.medium.com/one-objective-to-rule-them-all-1058e973bfc5) (Medium) — the mathematical argument for parsimony + whiskey funnel + Frodo/Samwise analogies.

#### Team OKRs vs individual — the anti-pattern

- [You Can't Handle OKRs](https://cwodtke.medium.com/you-cant-handle-okrs-5465cf161e81) (Medium) — cascading and individual OKR anti-patterns; prerequisites for OKRs.
