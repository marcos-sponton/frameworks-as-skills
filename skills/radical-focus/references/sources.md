# Radical Focus — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary text

- ***Radical Focus: Achieving Your Most Important Goals with Objectives and Key Results*** — Christina R. Wodtke. 1st edition 2016; **2nd expanded edition 2021 (+22,000 words).** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Radical-Focus-Achieving-Important-Objectives/dp/0996006028). The canonical source. Read it.

  Note: some sources reference a "3rd edition (2024)" but this could not be confirmed via [cwodtke.com](https://cwodtke.com/) at build time. If the 3rd edition exists, its deltas belong in `post-book.md`. Community PRs welcome.

## Other books by Christina Wodtke

- ***The Team That Managed Itself: A Story of Leadership*** — Christina Wodtke & Martin Eriksson, 2019. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Team-that-Managed-Itself-Leadership/dp/0996006060). Source of the **Team Health Monitor** concept and the extended treatment of team-level cadence + coaching + hiring alongside OKRs.
- ***Pencil Me In*** — Christina Wodtke, 2017. Visual thinking / sketching. Not OKR-focused, but part of her teaching context.
