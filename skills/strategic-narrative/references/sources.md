# Strategic Narrative — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary source (there is no book)

Andy Raskin has **no book** and **no Substack**. The primary source of the method is:

- **[The Greatest Sales Deck I've Ever Seen](https://medium.com/the-mission/the-greatest-sales-deck-ive-ever-seen-4f4ef3391ba0)** — Andy Raskin, Mission.org (Medium), September 2016. The Zuora deck breakdown. Established the 5-part framework. 2M+ views. The canonical anchor essay.

## Canonical follow-up essays (Medium)

Published in *Firm Narrative* (Raskin's own publication) and *Mission.org*. Cadence is bursty, not scheduled.

- [The Making of a Great Strategic Narrative](https://medium.com/the-mission/the-making-of-a-great-sales-narrative-978938b3926) — Uberflip case (Blockbuster → Netflix). Source of the Mike Latty *"Holy shit, I need this"* quote.
- [How Great Sales Pitches Drive Urgency](https://medium.com/the-mission/how-great-sales-pitches-drive-urgency-e35aa1069de3) — two urgency patterns (Name the Enemy + Undeniable Change). Source of *"How quickly can we get started?"* pattern-response.
- [This Is How a CEO Tells a Great Story](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/this-is-how-a-ceo-tells-a-great-story-5d158620cec9) — Tien Tzuo on Mad Money. Multi-channel narrative in action.
- [To Create a New Category, Name the New Game](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/to-create-a-new-category-name-the-new-game-70c2e55edc2e) — category creation via naming the game.
- [Is the "Greatest Sales Deck" Great for Savvy Prospects?](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/is-the-greatest-sales-deck-great-for-savvy-prospects-d5de48fb2f94) — post-2016 clarification. Setup is emotional engagement, not education.
- [Selling Your New Sales Deck to Sales](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/selling-your-new-sales-deck-to-sales-a7dc032a57bb) — rollout playbook. Zaius/Angoff, SpotMe discovery-opener, SkySlope broker validation.
- [Goodbye Product. Hello Movement.](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/goodbye-product-hello-movement-b06df08e02b8) — 2023 evolution. Movement > category.

## Referenced but not primary

External sources referenced by Raskin (used to attribute concepts he draws from):

- **Robert McKee** — *Story* (screenwriting). Source of *"what attracts human attention is change."*
- **Ben Horowitz** — the aphorism *"the company story is the company strategy"* that Raskin puts on his homepage.
- **Marc Benioff** — cited in the Salesforce case as the CEO who embodied the "End of Software" narrative.
- **Joseph Campbell** — *Hero with a Thousand Faces*. Raskin parallels his 5 parts to Campbell's Hero's Journey (Promised Land = reward; Magic Gifts = mentor's gift/talisman).

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **Complete chronological index of Raskin's Medium essays.** The 15-20 canonical ones are surfaced; some early or off-the-beaten-path essays are not.
- **Complete Lenny 2023 transcript.** The 19:27–29:18 5-part discussion is indexed; the rest of the episode is not.
- **Complete index of Raskin's own-show podcast episodes.** Only the Tien Tzuo episode is called out here.
- **Workshop materials.** Raskin's workshops (e.g., "Storytelling for Marketers") likely contain refinements not published elsewhere. Not public.
- **1:1 CEO engagement outputs.** By definition private. Cases essays are the public window.
- **Recent podcast appearances beyond mid-2023.** The list above is not exhaustive.
- **Community critiques and case studies of Raskin's method applied in the wild.** Practitioner adaptations often surface gotchas he doesn't.
