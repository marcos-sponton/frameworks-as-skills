# Strategic Narrative — Andy Raskin's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Andy Raskin publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and by topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay/post/podcast that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Raskin has no book and no Substack. His method lives in Medium essays + LinkedIn micro-essays + podcast appearances + his own site's podcast. Skipping his live sources = the skill drifts out of date. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (savvy-prospect setup, rollout to sales, category vs. movement, refresh after market shift), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/listen, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

## Structural note

Raskin's footprint is **case-first and non-schedule**. Some observations:

- **No book.** Multiple interviewers have asked. He has said the "book is the deck." The method lives in essays, not a bound volume.
- **No Substack.** He publishes to LinkedIn (highest cadence) and Medium (in bursts). The "newsletter" experience is following him on those two.
- **~15–20 canonical Medium essays** across the last decade. Bursts, not schedule.
- **Multiple LinkedIn posts per week.** Micro-essays (200–400 words) that expand a single point from the method, often triggered by a client engagement.
- **His own podcast** at andyraskin.com — CEO interviews, not method essays.

### Essays clave — the canonical ~15

Chronological + topical grouping.

#### The anchor essay

- [The Greatest Sales Deck I've Ever Seen](https://medium.com/the-mission/the-greatest-sales-deck-ive-ever-seen-4f4ef3391ba0) — Sep 2016, Mission.org. **The essay.** 2M+ views. The Zuora deck breakdown; established the 5-part framework.

#### Case studies as essays

- [The Making of a Great Strategic Narrative](https://medium.com/the-mission/the-making-of-a-great-sales-narrative-978938b3926) — Uberflip case. The "Blockbuster → Netflix" analogy applied to marketing tech. Includes the Mike Latty *"Holy shit, I need this"* quote.
- [This Is How a CEO Tells a Great Story](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/this-is-how-a-ceo-tells-a-great-story-5d158620cec9) — Tien Tzuo (Zuora) on Mad Money w/ Cramer. Shows the narrative running on TV, not just in the deck.

#### Method refinements

- [How Great Sales Pitches Drive Urgency](https://medium.com/the-mission/how-great-sales-pitches-drive-urgency-e35aa1069de3) — two urgency patterns: *Name the Enemy* + *Undeniable Change*. Also introduces the *"How quickly can we get started?"* pattern-response.
- [Is the "Greatest Sales Deck" Great for Savvy Prospects?](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/is-the-greatest-sales-deck-great-for-savvy-prospects-d5de48fb2f94) — post-2016 clarification. Setup is for emotional engagement, not education.
- [Goodbye Product. Hello Movement.](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/goodbye-product-hello-movement-b06df08e02b8) — 2023. Movement > category. Companion to the Pavilion 2023 talk.
- [To Create a New Category, Name the New Game](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/to-create-a-new-category-name-the-new-game-70c2e55edc2e) — category creation via game-naming, not category-naming.

#### Rollout / sales enablement

- [Selling Your New Sales Deck to Sales](https://medium.com/firm-narrative/selling-your-new-sales-deck-to-sales-a7dc032a57bb) — how to get the new deck adopted. Zaius/Angoff example. SpotMe discovery-opener pattern. Key rollout essay.
