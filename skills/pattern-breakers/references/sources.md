## Adjacent-author sources cited in this skill

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **Full audio transcripts of most *Starting Greatness* / *Pattern Breakers* podcast episodes** — only titles and select transcripts indexed here.
- **Peter Ziebelman's individual writing and Stanford course materials** — his voice in the book adds pedagogical structure; his solo material would enrich the skill.
- **A.G. Lafley–style deep interviews with the founders Maples names as archetypes** — Andreessen, Systrom, Krieger, Rachleff, etc., each have their own long bodies of work; only the Maples-facing intersection is consulted here.
- **Substack essays from the past few months** — cadence varies; new essays should be added as they appear.
- **Community critiques and applications of Pattern Breakers in the wild** — practitioners applying the framework often surface gotchas Maples himself doesn't name.
- **Non-English adaptations or international interviews.**

If you spot a source that would materially improve the skill, PR welcome (see repo README).
