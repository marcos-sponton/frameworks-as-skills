# Pattern Breakers — Mike Maples Jr's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Mike Maples Jr publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay/podcast/talk that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Pattern Breakers is not frozen in 2024. Maples continues publishing on Substack, the podcast, and Floodgate essays. Skipping his live sources = the skill drifts out of date. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (evaluating a pitch, deciding on a pivot, thinking about AI as an inflection, hunting for a founder-future-fit story), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/listen, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

## Primary source (essay stream) — Pattern Breakers Substack

**Live URL:** [https://patternbreakers.substack.com/](https://patternbreakers.substack.com/)

Launched alongside the 2024 book. Maples's live essay stream that extends the book to current situations (AI as inflection, incumbent vulnerability, founder mindset).

Entry-point essay: [*What are Pattern Breakers?*](https://patternbreakers.substack.com/p/what-are-pattern-breakers)

## Primary source (older essay archive) — Medium (@m2jr)

**Live URL:** [https://medium.com/@m2jr](https://medium.com/@m2jr)

Maples has been publishing on Medium since ~2013. Foundational essays that shaped the book:

### Backcasting and inflection identification

- [*How to build a breakthrough — the secret of Backcasting*](https://medium.com/@m2jr/how-to-build-a-breakthrough-3071b6415b06) — the anchor essay for the mental move. If the user needs to internalize backcasting vs. forecasting, this is the source.

### Heresy vs. contrarianism, breakthrough stress tests

- [*The Heresy of Breakthrough Startups*](https://medium.com/@m2jr/the-heresy-of-breakthrough-startups-289f12fb5930) — introduces heresy-vs-contrarianism and the Refusal/Heresy/Inflection/Demo four-question stress test.

### Movements and founder action

- [*Act different — Pattern-breaking founders don't just think different*](https://medium.com/@m2jr/act-different-b44a3d267eb4) — the action-side companion. Movements, storytelling (guide not hero), disagreeableness.

### Founder ambition and legendary companies

- [*Dare to make your startup Legendary*](https://medium.com/floodgate-fund/dare-to-make-your-startup-legendary-dc8eb68ba1fc) — Maples on aspiration and refusing the safe middle.

## Primary source (podcast) — Pattern Breakers (formerly Starting Greatness)

**Live URL:** [https://greatness.floodgate.com/](https://greatness.floodgate.com/) · **Apple Podcasts:** [Pattern Breakers](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/starting-greatness/id1488560647)

The podcast Maples hosts. Rebranded from *Starting Greatness* to *Pattern Breakers* alongside the 2024 book. Two formats: interviews with founders and operators (Marc Andreessen, Reid Hoffman, Kevin Systrom, Mike Krieger, Sarah Leary, and many more), and Maples's own solo "Breakthrough Lessons" episodes.

**Best entry points:**
- **Breakthrough Lessons: You Need a Breakthrough Insight** — [transcript](https://greatness.floodgate.com/episodes/lessons-of-greatness-you-need-a-breakthrough-insight-nyZEUscw/transcript). Maples solo on the definition of an insight; Rachleff's non-consensus-and-right.
- **Breakthrough Lessons: The One Thing That Matters Most** — [https://greatness.floodgate.com/episodes/breakthrough-lessons-the-one-thing-that-matters-most-yp_2_HCn](https://greatness.floodgate.com/episodes/breakthrough-lessons-the-one-thing-that-matters-most-yp_2_HCn).
- Andreessen interview episodes — the archetypal "living in the future" case.
- Kevin Systrom / Mike Krieger — Instagram origin story as pattern-break.

## Primary source (podcast appearances as guest)

Long-form conversational sources where Maples explains the framework at length. Best single episodes:

- **[Lenny's Newsletter — Pattern Breakers: How to find a great startup idea](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-find-a-great-startup-idea-mike-maples-jr)** (2024). Densest single conversational source that exists. Runs the full book framework with worked examples. Transcript available.
- **[Guy Kawasaki — Remarkable People — Mike Maples Jr.: Inflection Points and Startup Success](https://guykawasaki.com/mike-maples-jr-inflection-points-and-startup-success/)**. Maples explains the framework cleanly for a general audience.
- **[EUVC — Mike Maples Jr. on Inflection Theory and Breaking Patterns in European Venture](https://www.eu.vc/p/mike-maples-jr-on-inflection-theory)** (2024). Investor-side application; European market context.
- **Christopher Lochhead — Follow Your Different — episodes #163 and #366**. Long-form Maples on the framework and on breakthrough founders.
- **[Clearer Thinking Podcast — episode 226 — Pattern-breaking ideas, and startups that change the world](https://podcast.clearerthinking.org/episode/226/mike-maples-pattern-breaking-ideas-and-startups-that-change-the-world/)**. Philosophical framing of the framework.
