# Good Strategy Bad Strategy — Richard Rumelt's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Richard Rumelt publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them. Rumelt publishes less frequently than Martin (roughly monthly on Substack vs. Martin's weekly Medium), so the total volume is smaller, but each essay is dense.
>
> **Why it matters:** the kernel and the crux method are not frozen in 2011 and 2022. Rumelt continues to refine — most notably the "action agenda" reframe, the "beliefs" step between diagnosis and guiding policy, and continuing case analyses. Skipping his Substack = the skill drifts out of date.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (finding a crux, running a Foundry, dealing with a competitive shock, disruption analysis), scan this index for the matching essay and either (a) suggest they read it, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.
