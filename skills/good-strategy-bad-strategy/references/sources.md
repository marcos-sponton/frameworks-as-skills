# Good Strategy Bad Strategy — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts

- **Good Strategy Bad Strategy: The Difference and Why It Matters** — Richard Rumelt. Crown Business (US) / Profile Books (UK), 2011. [Amazon (US)](https://www.amazon.com/Good-Strategy-Bad-difference-matters/dp/0307886239). The canonical statement of the kernel. Read it.

- **The Crux: How Leaders Become Strategists** — Richard Rumelt. PublicAffairs, 2022. [Amazon (US)](https://www.amazon.com/Crux-Strategy-Grapples-Fundamental-Challenge/dp/1541701240). Extends the kernel with the crux method. Read this second.

## Rumelt's academic work (background, not skill source)

- **"How much does industry matter?"** — Rumelt, *Strategic Management Journal*, 1991. The paper showing firm-specific factors matter more than industry structure.
- **"Toward a Strategic Theory of the Firm"** — Rumelt, 1984. Founding paper of the Resource-Based View.
- **"Strategy, Structure, and Economic Performance"** — Rumelt, Harvard Business School, 1974. His PhD dissertation, still cited.
