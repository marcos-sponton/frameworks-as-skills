## Primary Channels (owned)

### robfitz.com — Blog
- **URL:** [https://robfitz.com/](https://robfitz.com/) (redirects from stuff.robfitz.com)
- **Cadence:** Irregular, ~75+ posts to date.
- **Content:** Essays on community-building, publishing, idea selection, product thinking, Outcome-Oriented Communities. Not Mom-Test-specific but extends the worldview.
- **Newsletter:** Subscribers get weekly updates when new content is published.

### usefulbooks.com — Write Useful Books hub
- **URL:** [https://www.usefulbooks.com/](https://www.usefulbooks.com/)
- **Content:** Resources for indie nonfiction authors. The book itself, plus guides on nonfiction design patterns, beta reading, self-publishing, and earning as an author.
- **Related tool:** Help This Book (helpthisbook.com) — a beta-reading tool.

### momtestbook.com — The Mom Test official page
- **URL:** [https://www.momtestbook.com/](https://www.momtestbook.com/)
- **Content:** Book summary, purchase links (paperback, Kindle, audiobook, PDF via Gumroad), language translations (Spanish, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Swedish, Russian, Serbian, Persian), course link, testimonials.

### YouTube — Rob Fitzpatrick
- **URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/c/RobFitzpatrick](https://www.youtube.com/c/RobFitzpatrick)
- **Cadence:** Irregular. ~6 videos covering Mom Test methodology, customer learning, self-publishing, Q&A sessions from Modern Author Academy (Winter 2024).
- **Notable:** Mom Test 2nd Edition beta reading announcement video (2025).
