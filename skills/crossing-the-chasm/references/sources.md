# Crossing the Chasm — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts — the eight books

- **Crossing the Chasm: Marketing and Selling High-Tech Products to Mainstream Customers** — Geoffrey A. Moore, HarperBusiness, **1991**; 2nd ed 1999; **3rd ed 2014 (canonical)**. [Book page](https://geoffreyamoore.com/book/crossing-the-chasm/). The foundational text.
- **Inside the Tornado: Marketing Strategies from Silicon Valley's Cutting Edge** — Geoffrey A. Moore, HarperBusiness, 1995. Post-chasm hypergrowth playbook. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_the_Tornado).
- **The Gorilla Game: Picking Winners in High Technology** — Geoffrey A. Moore, Paul Johnson, Tom Kippola, HarperBusiness, 1998. Investing lens.
- **Living on the Fault Line: Managing for Shareholder Value in the Age of the Internet** — Geoffrey A. Moore, HarperBusiness, 2000. CEO framework.
- **Dealing with Darwin: How Great Companies Innovate at Every Phase of Their Evolution** — Geoffrey A. Moore, Penguin Books, 2005. Core vs. Context.
- **Escape Velocity: Free Your Company's Future from the Pull of the Past** — Geoffrey A. Moore, HarperBusiness, 2011. Hierarchy of Powers.
- **Zone to Win: Organizing to Compete in an Age of Disruption** — Geoffrey A. Moore, Diversion Books, 2015. [Book page](https://geoffreyamoore.com/book/zone-to-win/). Four zones for incumbents.
- **The Infinite Staircase: What the Universe Tells Us About Life, Ethics, and Mortality** — Geoffrey A. Moore, BenBella Books, 2021. [Book page](https://geoffreyamoore.com/book/the-infinite-staircase/) · [BenBella](https://benbellabooks.com/shop/the-infinite-staircase/). Philosophy book.
