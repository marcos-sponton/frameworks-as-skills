# Crossing the Chasm — Geoffrey Moore's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Geoffrey Moore publishes new material regularly, or where his frameworks are canonically documented. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay / episode / talk that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Moore has been actively publishing for 35+ years — 8 books, hundreds of LinkedIn essays, blog posts, keynotes, and podcast appearances. The framework is stable, but the case bank and applied commentary (especially AI-era, 2024–2026) refresh constantly. A skill that references only the 1991 book goes stale. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (agentic AI adoption, Zone to Win session, after-the-chasm scaling, whole-product gap analysis), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/listen, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

### Highest-value blog posts (2024–2026)

Densely relevant for current chasm-crossing sessions.

- [**Can agentic AI 'cross the chasm' without falling in?**](https://geoffreyamoore.com/business_blogs/can-agentic-ai-cross-the-chasm-without-falling-in-tech-thought-leader-geoffrey-moore-assesses-progress-in-straddling-the-divide/) — Feb 6, 2026 (Diginomica-hosted). Moore's freshest framework-applied text. Central diagnostic: neither coding nor customer-service call centers yet possess enough trapped value for full re-engineering.
- [**The Real Future of AI**](https://geoffreyamoore.com/business_blogs/the-real-future-of-ai/) — Oct 2024. The four-stage adoption model + mission-critical re-engineering test + trapped-value variable.
- [**After the Chasm—Scaling Beyond the Beachhead**](https://geoffreyamoore.com/business_blogs/after-the-chasm-scaling-beyond-the-beachhead/) — 2024. Post-chasm Bowling Alley discipline; stay departmental; process-owner ally.
- [**Delighting your customers is bunk. Delivering on their desired outcomes is not.**](https://geoffreyamoore.com/business_blogs/delighting-your-customers-is-bunk-delivering-on-their-desired-outcomes-is-not/) — 2024. Sharpens compelling-reason-to-buy discipline against NPS-substitution drift.
- **More on Polarization — A Tale of Two Narratives** — 2024. Narrative-dynamics essay; less operational but voice-fidelity anchor.
