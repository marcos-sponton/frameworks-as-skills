## Amy Edmondson's books

- **The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological Safety in the Workplace for Learning, Innovation, and Growth** — Wiley, November 2018. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Fearless-Organization-Psychological-Workplace-Innovation/dp/1119477247)
- **Right Kind of Wrong: The Science of Failing Well** — Atria (US) / Cornerstone (UK), September 5, 2023. Winner of the Financial Times & Schroders Business Book of the Year 2023. [Simon & Schuster](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Right-Kind-of-Wrong/Amy-C-Edmondson/9781982195069)
- **Teaming: How Organizations Learn, Innovate, and Compete in the Knowledge Economy** — Jossey-Bass, 2012. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Teaming-Organizations-Innovate-Compete-Knowledge/dp/078649333X)
- **Teaming to Innovate** — Jossey-Bass, 2013.
- **Extreme Teaming: Lessons in Complex, Cross-Sector Leadership** — with Jean-François Harvey, Emerald, 2017.
