## Fearless Organization Scan — the productized diagnostic

**URL:** [fearlessorganizationscan.com](https://fearlessorganizationscan.com/)

The tool operationalizes the framework. Three levels:

- **Individual (free):** self-reflection scan.
- **Team PSI:** team-level assessment. **This is where PS actually lives.**
- **Enterprise PSI (EPSI):** org-wide measurement, with participation in the annual Global Benchmark.

**Annual Global Benchmark report** — published by fearlessorganizationscan.com. Cross-org PS data.

Companion site: **[fearlessorganizationscan.com/right-kind-of-wrong](https://fearlessorganizationscan.com/right-kind-of-wrong)** — the failure taxonomy applied.
