# Radical Candor — Post-Book Material

> The material Kim Scott has published *after* *Radical Candor* (2017/rev 2019) — nine years of refinements, extensions, and course-corrections. This is the differential of this skill: the base model knows the book; the skill knows what's happened since.

## Timeline

- **2017** — *Radical Candor: Be a Kick-Ass Boss Without Losing Your Humanity* — St. Martin's Press. NYT + WSJ bestseller.
- **2017–present** — *Radical Candor: Communication at Work* podcast launches (with Jason Rosoff and Amy Sandler). Weekly episodes. Continues.
- **2019** — Revised and expanded edition of *Radical Candor*. Adds the "Radical Candor is not brutal honesty" framing after ~2 years of watching the framework get weaponized.
- **2021** — *Just Work: How to Root Out Bias, Prejudice, and Bullying to Build a Kick-Ass Culture of Inclusivity* — St. Martin's Press. Introduces the BUL framework and the I/It/You response tools.
- **2024** — *Radical Respect: How to Work Together Better* — St. Martin's Griffin, May 7. The paperback release of *Just Work* is renamed and rewritten. 40,000 words shorter. Restructured to emphasize leader action rather than bystander/harmed-party action. Launched at SXSW 2024.
- **Ongoing** — Medium essays (@KimMaloneScott), Radical Candor company blog, LinkedIn posts, guest appearances on Lenny's Podcast, First Round Review, Jordan Harbinger, Intercom, Pathwise, Crisp, HBR IdeaCast.

## The Rebrand: Compassionate Candor

After watching Radical Candor get weaponized (using "radical" as license for bluntness while ignoring Care Personally), Kim has publicly floated **"Compassionate Candor"** as a softer alternative name. She hasn't fully replaced Radical Candor with it — the trademarked framework name stays — but she uses "Compassionate Candor" in newer materials as a hedge against the misinterpretation.

**Signal:** the fact that the author felt compelled to rebrand tells you how common the Obnoxious Aggression misapplication is. This skill's voice guard exists specifically because of this.

## Just Work → Radical Respect (2021 → 2024)

### What Just Work (2021) added to the Radical Candor system

Radical Candor covers the manager-report feedback dynamic. It does not, by itself, address the specific problems of bias, prejudice, and bullying — the structural failures where the Care Personally + Challenge Directly matrix isn't enough because the failure isn't a communication one, it's a power-and-identity one.

*Just Work* introduced:
- **BUL taxonomy** — Bias (not meaning it) / Prejudice (meaning it) / Bullying (being mean).
- **I / It / You statements** — the response tool for each category.
- **Roles by proximity** — person harmed, upstander, leader.
- **Leader escalation ladder** — Conversational → Compensation → Career consequence.
- **The rule:** *"Do not promote bullies. It's that simple."*

See `references/method.md` for the full BUL treatment.

### Why the 2024 rewrite

Kim published her own explanation ([Name Change: Radical Respect on Medium](https://kimmalonescott.medium.com/name-change-radical-respect-3bfd16dc31a2)):

- The paperback of *Just Work* released May 2024 as *Radical Respect*.
- 40,000 words cut. More than a third shorter.
- Restructured to move emphasis from bystanders and people harmed **to leaders**. Kim's reasoning: *"leaders need to lead if we are to solve the problems that get in the way of a respectful work environment."*
- Repositioned as a **prequel** to *Radical Candor* — you can't have Radical Candor at scale without Radical Respect as the substrate.

### The Radical Respect operating goals

The 2024 book frames the workplace operating system as two goals held simultaneously:

- **Optimize for collaboration, not coercion.**
- **Honor individuality, don't demand conformity.**

Definition of respect Kim uses:
> "A regard for the feelings, wishes, rights, and traditions of others" — owed to everyone by default, not earned.

Distinct from admiration-based respect. This distinction matters — "you have to earn my respect" is not respect in the sense Kim means it.

## The Podcast — Radical Candor: Communication at Work (2017–present)

**This is the freshest, densest single source of Kim's post-book thinking.** Weekly cadence, with Jason Rosoff (co-founder) and Amy Sandler (host). Topic-specific episodes covering:

- Applying the 2×2 to specific scenarios (giving feedback to your boss, feedback across cultures, feedback in remote/hybrid teams, feedback in high-stress environments).
- The GSD wheel in depth (season 4 episode 2 covers it explicitly: [Use the GSD Wheel So No One Feels Sad, Bad or Left Out](https://www.radicalcandor.com/podcast/s4-e2-the-gsd-wheel)).
- Career Conversations episodes (season 1 episode 5: [Career Conversations](https://www.radicalcandor.com/podcast/s1-e5-career-conversations)). Multiple follow-ups with Russ Laraway.
- Just Work / Radical Respect deep-dives after the book launches.
- Listener questions and applied scenarios.

**Editorial pattern:** Kim delivers the framework, Jason offers pushback / additional cases, Amy hosts and translates. The three-voice format models Radical Candor itself — they disagree in public, kindly, without softening.

## Where Kim's thinking has evolved most since 2017

- **Weaponization awareness** — the 2017 book underplays how often Radical Candor gets used as permission for Obnoxious Aggression. The 2019 revision, the "Not Brutal Honesty" essay, and the "Compassionate Candor" rebrand all respond to this. **This skill's voice guard exists specifically because Kim's own post-book work has been dominated by this concern.**
- **Structural / DEI extension** — Just Work / Radical Respect extends Kim's operating system from the manager-report dyad to the whole workplace, addressing the bias/prejudice/bullying failures that Radical Candor alone doesn't fix.
- **Leader emphasis** — the 2024 Radical Respect rewrite explicitly shifts responsibility from bystanders / harmed parties to leaders. Kim's mature position: the system-level failures require system-level intervention, and only leaders can design that.
- **Cross-cultural calibration** — podcast episodes address how the framework applies (or fails) in different national/regional cultures. The axes are universal; the calibration of what counts as "direct" is not.
- **Remote/hybrid application** — post-2020 episodes address how HHIPP survives (Immediate becomes harder, In-Person becomes video, Private/Public becomes harder to control).
- **Deep partnership with Russ Laraway on Career Conversations** — Kim consistently credits Russ. His 2022 book *When They Win, You Win* is the deeper treatment of Career Conversations and adjacent methods.

## What Kim has NOT changed her mind about

- The 2×2 is unchanged. Care Personally + Challenge Directly, four quadrants, trademarked names. This is stable.
- HHIPP is unchanged.
- The Bob story is unchanged as the canonical Ruinous Empathy warning.
- The Sheryl "um" story is unchanged as the canonical Radical Candor illustration.
- The rule that Care Personally is load-bearing has only intensified — Kim defends it harder now than she did in 2017.
- "Do not promote bullies. It's that simple." — non-negotiable from Just Work onward.

- **When the topic is weaponization or the "just being honest" defense** — the [Radical Candor Is Not Brutal Honesty](https://kimmalonescott.medium.com/radical-candor-is-not-brutal-honesty-6cdeb04cac53) essay + the Compassionate Candor rebrand.
- **When the topic is bias / prejudice / bullying / DEI** — Radical Respect (2024), not the 2017 book. Cite the newer taxonomy and I/It/You statements.
- **When the topic is a specific applied scenario** (feedback across cultures, in remote teams, to your boss, in a high-stress startup) — the podcast archive.
- **When the topic is Career Conversations** — cite Russ Laraway explicitly, point at his book *When They Win, You Win*, and the podcast episode with Kim.
- **When the topic is running better meetings / decisions** — the GSD wheel content, especially the season 4 episode 2.
