# Thinking in Bets — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts (the three books)

- **Thinking in Bets: Making Smarter Decisions When You Don't Have All the Facts** — Annie Duke. Portfolio (Penguin), 2018. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Thinking-Bets-Making-Smarter-Decisions/dp/0735216355). The frame.
- **How to Decide: Simple Tools for Making Better Choices** — Annie Duke. Portfolio, 2020. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/How-Decide-Simple-Making-Better/dp/0593418484). The toolkit.
- **Quit: The Power of Knowing When to Walk Away** — Annie Duke. Portfolio, 2022. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Quit-Power-Knowing-When-Walk/dp/0593422996). The counter-force.

## Related books (Duke cites or is cited alongside)

- **Kahneman, Daniel — *Thinking, Fast and Slow*** (2011). Duke's direct intellectual ancestor.
- **Klein, Gary — *Sources of Power: How People Make Decisions*** (1998). Source of the premortem.
- **Tetlock, Philip & Gardner — *Superforecasting: The Art and Science of Prediction*** (2015). Calibration research; Alliance forecasting tournaments modeled on Good Judgment Project.
- **Duckworth, Angela — *Grit: The Power of Passion and Perseverance*** (2016). Duke positions *Quit* as counter-force.

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **Full-text verification of the three books.** The dossier explicitly flagged this — most quotes came through summary sources. Verifying quotes against print editions (with page numbers) is the single highest-value contribution.
- **Alliance for Decision Education podcast episode index by topic.** Only a couple episodes referenced by number.
- **First Round Capital protocol details** beyond what Lenny 2024 surfaced — the 1–7 scale, specific dimensions, calibration cadence.
- **Duke's academic work** at Penn under Barbara Mellers — cognitive-psych papers she published before the poker career.
- **Additional Substack essays** beyond the illustrative recent ones — a topic-tagged full archive would improve retrieval.
- **YouTube keynote lectures beyond TEDxGeorgetown / SpeakInc** — several exist but are not indexed here.
- **Recent podcast appearances 2025–2026** beyond Freakonomics update.
- **Community critiques and applications** — practitioners applying Duke's toolkit in the wild often surface gotchas Duke doesn't.
- **A.G. Lafley writing on decision-making** — not Duke-adjacent by attribution, but overlapping in the P&G / consumer-goods space.
