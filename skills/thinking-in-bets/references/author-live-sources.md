# Thinking in Bets — Annie Duke's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Annie Duke publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and by topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay / video / podcast that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Duke's toolkit is not frozen in 2018 (or 2020, or 2022). She writes on Substack, hosts the Alliance podcast, appears on 1–2 external podcasts per quarter, and shapes First Round Capital's decision protocol in real time. Skipping the live sources = the skill drifts out of date.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (post-mortem, kill criteria decision, group decision hygiene, quit-or-not, VC-founder decision), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/watch it, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

## Fuente principal (organization) — Alliance for Decision Education

**Live URL:** [https://alliancefordecisioneducation.org/](https://alliancefordecisioneducation.org/)

- 501(c)(3) that Duke co-founded and chairs.
- Duke's official title: *"Alliance Co-founder, Author, and Decision-Making Expert."*
- Mission: bring Decision Education to K-12 classrooms. *"Teach students how to think, not what to think."*

**Programs:**
- **Standards & Crosswalks** — decision-making standards mapped to state curricula.
- **Decision Education Incubator** — teacher training / curriculum development.
- **Student Forecasting Tournaments** — modeled on Tetlock's Good Judgment Project.
- **Lunch & Learn webinars** — free public sessions.
- **Research grants** — funded decision-education research.

**Duke's Alliance page:** [https://alliancefordecisioneducation.org/learn/about-the-alliance/team/annie-duke/](https://alliancefordecisioneducation.org/learn/about-the-alliance/team/annie-duke/)
