# 7 Powers — Hamilton Helmer's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Hamilton Helmer publishes new material or appears in long-form.
>
> **Important note on shape:** Helmer's live footprint is **podcast-heavy, not essay-heavy.** Unlike Roger Martin (~260 Medium essays) or Richard Rumelt (books + occasional podcasts), Helmer has **no Substack, no Medium, no newsletter, no blog cadence, no Twitter/X presence, and no regular LinkedIn posting**. His public output since 2016 is: the Strategy Capital site, ~8 podcast appearances every 12–18 months, and one recurring interview partner (Chenyi Shi) working on the "Second Invention" book.
>
> **Why this matters:** the skill's freshness depends almost entirely on catching new podcast appearances as they happen. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current. When a new Helmer interview drops anywhere, add it here.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (moat analysis, sequencing question, second-invention pattern), scan this index for the matching episode and either (a) suggest they listen to it, or (b) WebFetch the transcript inline if depth is needed.
