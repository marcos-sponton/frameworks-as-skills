## Patrick Lencioni's books

- **The Five Temptations of a CEO** — Jossey-Bass, 1998. Precursor. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Five-Temptations-CEO-Anniversary/dp/047026758X)
- **The Four Obsessions of an Extraordinary Executive** — Jossey-Bass, 2000. Introduces org health.
- **The Five Dysfunctions of a Team: A Leadership Fable** — Jossey-Bass, 2002 (20th Anniversary Edition 2022). [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Five-Dysfunctions-Team-Leadership-Fable/dp/0787960756) · [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-jp/The+Five+Dysfunctions+of+a+Team:+A+Leadership+Fable,+20th+Anniversary+Edition-p-9780787960759)
- **Death by Meeting** — Jossey-Bass, 2004.
- **Overcoming the Five Dysfunctions of a Team: A Field Guide** — Jossey-Bass, 2005. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Overcoming-Five-Dysfunctions-Team-Facilitators/dp/0787976377) · [Table Group](https://www.tablegroup.com/product/overcoming-the-five-dysfunctions-a-field-guide/)
- **Silos, Politics and Turf Wars** — Jossey-Bass, 2006. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Silos-Politics-Turf-Wars-Competitors/dp/0787976385)
- **The Truth About Employee Engagement** (orig. *The Three Signs of a Miserable Job*) — Jossey-Bass, 2007.
- **Getting Naked** — Jossey-Bass, 2010.
- **The Advantage: Why Organizational Health Trumps Everything Else in Business** — Jossey-Bass, 2012. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Advantage-Organizational-Health-Everything-Business/dp/0470941529)
- **The Ideal Team Player: How to Recognize and Cultivate the Three Essential Virtues** — Jossey-Bass, 2016. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Ideal-Team-Player-Recognize-Cultivate/dp/1119209595)
- **The Motive: Why So Many Leaders Abdicate Their Most Important Responsibilities** — Matt Holt Books, 2020.
- **The 6 Types of Working Genius** — Matt Holt Books, 2022. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Types-Working-Genius-Understand-Frustrations/dp/1637743297)
