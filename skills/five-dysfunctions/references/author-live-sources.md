# Patrick Lencioni — Live Sources Index

> Index of every place Patrick Lencioni and The Table Group publish regularly. This is the differential of a live skill — a Five Dysfunctions skill that doesn't index live sources goes stale fast. Lencioni is a high-cadence operator: weekly podcast, continuous book publication (~one every 2–3 years since 1998), a firm with its own product/consulting practice, and a newer product line (Working Genius) with its own hub.

## Books (chronological, canonical)

| Year | Title | Publisher | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1998 | **The Five Temptations of a CEO** | Jossey-Bass | Precursor. Five temptations that cause CEOs to fail. The lowest three seed the pyramid. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Five-Temptations-CEO-Anniversary/dp/047026758X) |
| 2000 | **The Four Obsessions of an Extraordinary Executive** | Jossey-Bass | Introduces "organizational health" and the Four Disciplines (later expanded in *The Advantage*). |
| 2002 (rev 2022) | **The Five Dysfunctions of a Team: A Leadership Fable** | Jossey-Bass | The canonical pyramid book. 20th Anniversary Edition adds updated foreword. 3M+ copies. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Five-Dysfunctions-Team-Leadership-Fable/dp/0787960756) |
| 2004 | **Death by Meeting** | Jossey-Bass | Meeting-craft. Four meeting types. |
| 2005 | **Overcoming the Five Dysfunctions of a Team: A Field Guide** | Jossey-Bass | Practitioner companion with tools + exercises. [Table Group](https://www.tablegroup.com/product/overcoming-the-five-dysfunctions-a-field-guide/) |
| 2006 | **Silos, Politics and Turf Wars** | Jossey-Bass | Cross-functional dysfunction. "Thematic goal." |
| 2007 | **The Truth About Employee Engagement** (originally *The Three Signs of a Miserable Job*) | Jossey-Bass | Manager-report layer. Anonymity, irrelevance, immeasurement. |
| 2010 | **Getting Naked** | Jossey-Bass | Vulnerability in consulting client relationships. |
| 2012 | **The Advantage: Why Organizational Health Trumps Everything Else in Business** | Jossey-Bass | **First non-fable.** Consolidates the operating system. Four Disciplines + Six Critical Questions. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Advantage-Organizational-Health-Everything-Business/dp/0470941529) |
| 2016 | **The Ideal Team Player: How to Recognize and Cultivate the Three Essential Virtues** | Jossey-Bass | Humble + hungry + smart. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Ideal-Team-Player-Recognize-Cultivate/dp/1119209595) |
| 2020 | **The Motive: Why So Many Leaders Abdicate Their Most Important Responsibilities** | Matt Holt Books | Reward-centered vs. responsibility-centered leadership. |
| 2022 | **The 6 Types of Working Genius: A Better Way to Understand Your Gifts, Your Frustrations, and Your Team** | Matt Holt Books | WIDGET framework. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Types-Working-Genius-Understand-Frustrations/dp/1637743297) |

## Company (The Table Group)

- **Founded:** 1997 in the Bay Area.
- **President:** Patrick Lencioni.
- **What they do:** executive team consulting, org-health consulting, workshops (many aligned to specific book frameworks), speaker engagements, published assessments, Working Genius certification.
- **Consulting practice model:** small team of principal consultants (Cody Thompson is one; others rotate through the podcast). Lencioni himself takes select client engagements.
- **Working Genius certification** is the newer scalable channel — practitioners get certified to run the WIDGET assessment and workshops.
