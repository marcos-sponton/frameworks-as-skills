# Play Bigger — Sources

> Complete traceability. Every claim in this skill traces back to a source in this file. Multi-author framework — attribution to specific books, authors, and dates matters.

## Founding book

- **Al Ramadan, Dave Peterson, Christopher Lochhead, Kevin Maney** — *Play Bigger: How Pirates, Dreamers, and Innovators Create and Dominate Markets — A Silicon Valley Guide to Category Design for Building Legendary Companies* (HarperBusiness, 2016). [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Play-Bigger-Dreamers-Innovators-Dominate/dp/0062407619) · [UK edition](https://www.amazon.com/Play-Bigger-Innovators-Categories-Dominate/dp/0349411360) subtitled *How Rebels and Innovators Create New Categories and Dominate Markets*.

## Primary research

- **Play Bigger — Time to Market Cap (TTMC) Report.** The primary-research paper behind the "76% of category market cap goes to one company" statistic. [Play Bigger site](https://www.playbigger.com/time-to-market-cap-report) · [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/playbigger/play-bigger-time-to-market-cap-100-report).
- **HBR (Jan–Feb 2016) — Category Design article** — Ramadan / Peterson / Lochhead / Maney summary article of the book's thesis.

## Author backgrounds — quick reference

- **Al Ramadan** — Founder & CEO, Category Design Agency. ~30 years exec (Adobe, Macromedia, Quokka Sports — CTO for an America's Cup campaign, founded an Australian software company). [Category Design Agency team page](https://www.categorydesignadvisors.com/) · Time Magazine "most influential in the digital economy."
- **Christopher Lochhead** — "Godfather of Category Design." Former 3x public tech company CMO. 14x #1 bestselling Amazon author. Hosts Follow Your Different + Lochhead on Marketing. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christopherlochhead).
- **Kevin Maney** — Bestselling author, Newsweek columnist, partner at Category Design Agency. Author of *The Two-Second Advantage* (w/ TIBCO CEO Vivek Ranadivé, NYT bestseller) and *The Maverick and His Machine: Thomas Watson, Sr. and the Making of IBM*. [Newsweek profile](https://www.newsweek.com/authors/kevin-maney) · [kevinmaney.com](https://kevinmaney.com/about) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevinmaney/).
- **Dave Peterson** — Co-founder, Play Bigger. 20+ years running comms/CMO at Vantive, Mercury Interactive, Aggregate Knowledge, Coverity. Co-founded (and shut down) GiveMeTalk!, a podcasting pioneer. Graduate of the University of Northern Iowa. [LinkedIn / Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/dave-peterson).
