# Play Bigger — Author live sources

This is the file that has to be kept fresh — Lochhead and Category Pirates publish multiple times per week, so this ages fast. **Community PRs to this file keep the skill from going stale.** When the user's situation matches a specific essay / video / podcast, consult this index and either point them to it or WebFetch the URL for depth.

## The four co-authors of *Play Bigger* (2016)

### Al Ramadan — Founder & CEO, Category Design Agency

- **Primary org:** [Category Design Agency (formerly Play Bigger Advisors)](https://www.categorydesignadvisors.com/) — Ramadan is Founder & CEO. Case studies, TTMC research, current productized version of the method.
- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alramadan/)** — moderate cadence; announcements, category-design essays, book announcements.
- **Podcast appearances (highest signal):**
  - [Roger Dooley #117 — Play Bigger to Dominate Your Market with Al Ramadan](https://www.rogerdooley.com/ep-117-play-bigger-dominate-market-al-ramadan/).
  - [Follow Your Different ep 285 — How Category Contenders Become Category Leaders with Al Ramadan](https://lochhead.com/fyd-how-category-contenders-become-category-leaders-with-al-ramadan/).
  - [Follow Your Different ep 195 — From Category Contenders to Category Kings with Al Ramadan](https://lochhead.com/lom-al-ramadan-category-contenders-to-category-kings/).
  - [Category Thinkers — A Call To Action For Category Designers with Play Bigger Co-authors Lochhead, Ramadan, and Maney](https://www.categorythinkers.com/podcast/call-to-action-play-bigger) — rare three-co-author reunion.
- **Books co-authored:** *Play Bigger* (2016), *The Existing Market Trap* (2025).
- **Background:** ~30 years executive experience (Adobe, Macromedia, Quokka Sports — CTO for an America's Cup campaign, founded an Australian software company). Named by Time Magazine as one of the most influential people in the digital economy.

### Christopher Lochhead — "Godfather of Category Design"

**The loudest and most-published of the four post-book.** Runs two podcasts, multiple sites, near-daily LinkedIn, drives most of the Category Pirates output.

- **Primary site:** [lochhead.com](https://lochhead.com/) — book archive, essay hub, podcast index. Best single starting point for anyone new to the method.
- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christopherlochhead)** — daily / near-daily. Highest cadence provocateur voice.
- **Podcasts:**
  - **[Follow Your Different™](https://lochhead.com/follow-your-different/)** — ~450 episodes as of 2026. "Top 5 business podcast" framing. Dialogue-podcast format, category-design-forward, deliberately provocative. Weekly or more. Notable episodes:
    - Ep 285 — [How Category Contenders Become Category Leaders w/ Al Ramadan](https://lochhead.com/fyd-how-category-contenders-become-category-leaders-with-al-ramadan/).
    - Ep 187 — [How To Become A Category Pirate | Lenny Rachitsky guest ep](https://lochhead.com/how-to-become-a-category-pirate-with-lenny-rachitsky/).
    - Ep 415 — [Out Of The Existing Market Trap (2025)](https://lochhead.com/fyd-out-of-the-exisitng-trap-christopher-lochhead-2025/) — the 2025 refresh; bolt-on AI critique.
  - **[Lochhead on Marketing](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/lochhead-on-marketing/id1475593214)** — 220+ eps. Weekly. Shorter, marketing-focused, more solo-teach mode.
- **Books:** *Play Bigger* (2016), *Niche Down* (2018 w/ Heather Clancy), *The Existing Market Trap* (2025 w/ Ramadan + Wellcome + Grice), + every Category Pirates book.

- **Primary site:** [kevinmaney.com](https://kevinmaney.com/about).
- **[Newsweek column](https://www.newsweek.com/authors/kevin-maney)** — weekly technology + society. Journalist voice; not category-design-forward every week, but consistently technology-market coverage that maps back to the method.
- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevinmaney/)** — moderate cadence.
- **[Muck Rack](https://muckrack.com/kevin-maney/articles)** — journalism archive across USA Today, Fortune, Fast Company, Wired.
- **Books:** *Play Bigger* (2016), *The Two-Second Advantage* (w/ TIBCO CEO Vivek Ranadivé — NYT bestseller), *The Maverick and His Machine: Thomas Watson, Sr. and the Making of IBM*, plus other tech books.
- **Podcast appearances:**
  - [Category Thinkers — A Call To Action For Category Designers w/ Lochhead, Ramadan, Maney](https://www.categorythinkers.com/podcast/call-to-action-play-bigger).

### Dave Peterson — Co-founder, Play Bigger

- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daveptrs/)** and **[X/Twitter — @daveptrs](https://twitter.com/daveptrs)** — lower cadence.
- **[Crunchbase profile](https://www.crunchbase.com/person/dave-peterson)** — background.
