## Goldratt's own books (chronological)

- **1984 — *The Goal: A Process of Ongoing Improvement*** (w/ Jeff Cox). Revised editions 1986, 1992, 2004, 2014. [North River Press](https://northriverpress.com/) · [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Goal-Process-Ongoing-Improvement/dp/0884271951)
- **1986 — *The Race*** (w/ Robert E. Fox). North River Press.
- **1990 — *The Haystack Syndrome: Sifting Information Out of the Data Ocean***. North River Press.
- **1990 — *What Is This Thing Called Theory of Constraints and How Should It Be Implemented?***. North River Press.
- **1994 — *It's Not Luck***. North River Press. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Its-Not-Luck-Production-Constraints/dp/1665157577)
- **1997 — *Critical Chain***. North River Press.
- **2000 — *Necessary But Not Sufficient*** (w/ Eli Schragenheim + Carol A. Ptak). North River Press. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Necessary-But-Not-Sufficient-Constraints/dp/0566084503)
- **2002 — *Essays on the Theory of Constraints***. North River Press.
- **2003 — *Late Night Discussions on the Theory of Constraints***. North River Press.
- **2003 — *Production: The TOC Way*** (revised).
- **2005 — *Beyond the Goal: Eliyahu Goldratt Speaks on the Theory of Constraints***. Your Coach in a Box (audiobook).
- **2006 — *Isn't It Obvious?*** (w/ Ilan Eshkoli + Joe Brown Leer). North River Press.
- **2008 — *The Choice*** (w/ Efrat Goldratt-Ashlag). **Revised Edition 2023.** [Routledge](https://www.routledge.com/The-Choice/Goldratt-Goldratt-Ashlag/p/book/9781032445151)
