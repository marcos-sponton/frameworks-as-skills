# Theory of Constraints — Live Sources (Stewardship Map)

> **Important framing.** Eliyahu Goldratt died June 11, 2011. There is no live Goldratt Substack, Medium, or podcast. The "live sources" for TOC are the stewards who carry the framework forward — the Goldratt family (Rami, Efrat), the institutional bodies (Goldratt Consulting, TOCICO), the multimedia archive of Goldratt's own voice (TOC.tv), and the independent extenders publishing new work in TOC's lineage (Gene Kim / IT Revolution, Clarke Ching, Steve Tendon, Carol Ptak).
>
> Refresh cadence for this file: **quarterly**. Efrat's book cadence, TOCICO's annual conference cycle, and Kim/Ching/Tendon publishing all move on a 3–6 month rhythm.

- Video: "Goldratt's Rules of Flow — My Story Behind the Book" (YouTube; search for the title)
- **Refresh signal:** new book announcements from North River Press / Routledge; TOCICO conference keynote lineups.

### North River Press
- Publisher of every Goldratt novel; still in print.
- https://northriverpress.com/
- Distributes: *The Goal*, *It's Not Luck*, *Critical Chain*, *Necessary But Not Sufficient*, *Isn't It Obvious?*, *The Choice*, *Goldratt's Rules of Flow*.
- **Refresh signal:** new-release announcements (Efrat cadence is the main signal).

### The Goldratt Institute (Avraham Y. Goldratt Institute)
- Founded 1986 as the original TOC training body. Goldratt broke with it in the 1990s.
- Continues under separate leadership; useful historical context but not part of the current family-led stewardship.

## What is NOT a live source

- **Eliyahu Goldratt on LinkedIn / Substack / Medium / Twitter** — he died in 2011. Anyone posting as "Goldratt" today is not him.
- **Third-party TOC blogs of unknown provenance** — the TOC space has attracted a wide range of quality. Use the stewards above as the trust filter; treat third-party interpretations as *applications*, not canonical TOC.
- **Old Goldratt Institute material** — from before his 1990s split with the Institute; historically interesting but not aligned with the family-led stewardship track.
