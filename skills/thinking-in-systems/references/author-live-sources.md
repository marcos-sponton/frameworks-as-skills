# Thinking in Systems — Donella Meadows's sources (archive + lineage)

> **Live sources** = places where new material appears regularly. **Donella Meadows died in February 2001. She has produced no new material since.** So this file indexes:
>
> 1. The **archive** — [The Donella Meadows Project (donellameadows.org)](https://donellameadows.org/) — a comprehensive, stable archive of her writing, run as a project of the Academy for Systems Change.
> 2. The **organizational continuation** — [Academy for Systems Change](https://academyforchange.org/) (formerly the Sustainability Institute Meadows founded in 1996). Where the method is applied and extended by living practitioners.
> 3. **Meadows-lineage practitioners** publishing today — Diana Wright, Dennis Meadows, Sara Schley, Linda Booth Sweeney.
> 4. **Adjacent publications** carrying the tradition — The Systems Thinker (Senge / MIT lineage, includes archived Meadows).
> 5. **Modern descendants** operationalizing Meadows for the 21st century — Kate Raworth (Doughnut Economics), Johan Rockström (planetary boundaries), the degrowth movement.
>
> **What does NOT exist and must not be fabricated:** Meadows has no Substack, no X/Twitter, no LinkedIn, no Mastodon, no podcast, no active blog. Any of those attributed to her personally are false. She died in 2001.
>
> **Why this matters:** most Meadows references in the wild are shallow — 12 Leverage Points as a bare list, "systems thinking" without archetypes. This file's purpose is to point the assistant at the actual archive and the actual lineage so the skill produces work with real grounding, not just parroted concepts.

## The primary archive — donellameadows.org

**Live URL:** [https://donellameadows.org/](https://donellameadows.org/) — The Donella Meadows Project. A project of the Academy for Systems Change (formerly Sustainability Institute / Donella Meadows Institute).

**Structure:**

### Global Citizen columns
Meadows's syndicated weekly newspaper column, published from **1985 to her death in 2001**. Approximately 800 columns applying systems thinking to public issues — environment, sustainability, consumption, food systems, climate, policy, everyday sense-making. **The best single source for Meadows in public voice** — humane, essayistic, warm about people, precise about systems.

The columns are what everyone should read before writing anything in Meadows's voice. Access via the donellameadows.org archive, tagged by topic (agriculture, climate change, sustainability, and others).

### Dear Folks Letters
Personal / community correspondence from Meadows, largely from her time at Cobb Hill (1996–2001). Less systematized than the columns; personal register.

### Articles & Papers
- **[Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System (1999)](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/)** — the canonical essay. The single most-cited Meadows text.
- **[Dancing with Systems](https://donellameadows.org/archives/dancing-with-systems/)** — the humility companion essay.
- **[Places to Intervene in a System — Whole Earth (1997)](https://designsciencelab.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/PlacesInterveneSystem-Meadows.pdf)** — the earlier condensed version.
- Academic and research papers from her Dartmouth and Sustainability Institute periods.

### Books
- ***Thinking in Systems: A Primer*** (Chelsea Green, 2008) — posthumous, edited by Diana Wright.
- ***The Limits to Growth*** (1972) — with Dennis Meadows, Jørgen Randers, William Behrens III.
- ***Beyond the Limits*** (1992) — with Dennis Meadows and Randers.
- ***Limits to Growth: The 30-Year Update*** (2004) — with Dennis Meadows and Randers, published posthumously with her earlier contributions.

### Media
- **In a World of Systems** — multimedia project narrated / illustrated by David Macaulay in collaboration with Linda Booth Sweeney. Systems thinking for a general audience.
- Historical lectures at Dartmouth and elsewhere (fragmentary; scattered on YouTube via third-party uploads).

### Systems Thinking Resources
The [Systems Thinking Resources page](https://donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-resources/) features:
- The Iceberg Model (downloadable).
- The Bathtub Theorem (with Climate Interactive simulation).
- Stock and Flow Diagramming tools.
- Guided Envisioning of a Sustainable World.

### Essays by Colleagues
Contributions from other writers extending Meadows's work.

### Update cadence
**The archive is stable.** Meadows died in 2001; no new material from her appears. The site adds occasional colleague essays and curated collections but is not a live blog. **Community PRs to this skill should focus on adding Meadows-lineage practitioners (below), not on tracking donellameadows.org for new Meadows posts.**

## The organizational continuation — Academy for Systems Change

**Live URL:** [https://academyforchange.org/](https://academyforchange.org/) — organizational continuation of the Sustainability Institute Meadows founded in 1996.

**What it does:**
- Supports community leaders globally in systems change work.
- Runs practitioner programs and Fellowship / Fellows programs.
- Publishes the **Systems Fieldbook** — a guide to compelling questions in systems change practice.
- Hosts **Field Stories** — case studies of systems change initiatives (e.g., Baja California Sur, Mississippi River basin).
- Maintains the **Donella Meadows Project** as one of its initiatives.

**Base:** Burlington, Vermont (PO Box 4593).

**This is where Meadows's method is applied by living practitioners today.** It's the closest thing to a "live source" for Meadows-lineage material.

## Meadows-lineage practitioners publishing today

Verified as of 2026. **Community PRs welcome to add or update.**

### Diana Wright

Colleague of Meadows at the Sustainability Institute. **Editor of *Thinking in Systems: A Primer*** (2008). The person who assembled the posthumous book from Meadows's ~1993 drafts. Central custodian of the textual legacy.

### Dennis Meadows

Donella's ex-husband and co-author of *The Limits to Growth* (1972), *Beyond the Limits* (1992), and *Limits to Growth: The 30-Year Update* (2004). Continues to speak and write on planetary limits, systems dynamics, and policy. Former director of the Institute for Policy and Social Science Research at the University of New Hampshire.

Co-created the **Balaton Group** (1982) with Donella — an international network of sustainability practitioners still active today.

### Sara Schley

**SEED Systems** and the SoL Sustainability Consortium. Taught at the Sustainability Institute Fellows Program at Cobb Hill. Extends Meadows's method into organizational learning contexts.

### Linda Booth Sweeney

Systems-thinking educator. Collaborator on the **In a World of Systems** multimedia project with David Macaulay. Author of systems-thinking curriculum for children and adults. Focus on systems literacy as accessible practice.

### Academy for Systems Change Fellows

Rotating cohort of practitioners. Names change year to year. Community PRs welcome to note specific Fellows publishing significant work.

## Modern descendants — extending Meadows into 21st-century contexts

These are **not Meadows's own work.** They are direct methodological descendants. When invoked in a Meadows session, attribute correctly.

### Kate Raworth — Doughnut Economics

Book: ***Doughnut Economics: Seven Ways to Think Like a 21st-Century Economist*** (Random House, 2017). Concept dates from 2012 Oxfam paper.

Raworth **explicitly credits Meadows.** The "doughnut" frames economic policy as needing to stay above a social foundation (meeting human needs) while remaining below an ecological ceiling (planetary boundaries). Direct operationalization of Meadows's balancing-loop analysis for global economic policy.

Live footprint:
- **Doughnut Economics Action Lab (DEAL):** [doughnuteconomics.org](https://doughnuteconomics.org/)
- **Book:** widely available.
- **Speaking / writing:** ongoing.

### Johan Rockström — Planetary Boundaries

*Planetary Boundaries* framework, published by Rockström and colleagues at the **Stockholm Resilience Centre** in 2009. Identifies nine biophysical thresholds the Earth system operates within safely.

Direct methodological descent from *The Limits to Growth*. Where Meadows and colleagues used World3 to argue that limits existed, Rockström operationalized specific limits for the Anthropocene.

Live footprint:
- **Stockholm Resilience Centre:** [stockholmresilience.org](https://www.stockholmresilience.org/)
- Multiple papers, TED talks, ongoing science.

### Degrowth movement

Political-economic extension of Meadows's paradigm-level (leverage point 2) critique of the growth paradigm.

Key authors:
- **Jason Hickel** — *Less Is More: How Degrowth Will Save the World* (Penguin, 2020).
- **Giorgos Kallis** — *Degrowth* (Agenda Publishing, 2018).
- **Serge Latouche** — foundational degrowth theorist.
- **Kate Soper** — *Post-Growth Living* (2020).

Most degrowth authors don't cite Meadows directly, but the intellectual lineage is direct.

### Peter Senge / Otto Scharmer / Presencing Institute

Adjacent tradition. Senge's *The Fifth Discipline* (1990) shares MIT system-dynamics roots with Meadows and formalized the systems archetypes for organizational learning. Otto Scharmer's Theory U extends Senge into leadership and change practice.

**Note the tension:** Dave Snowden (Cynefin) has publicly critiqued Senge's *Learning Organization* as imposing homogeneity of values. That critique does not neatly transfer to Meadows herself. See [[cynefin]] cross-reference.

Live footprint:
- **Society for Organizational Learning (SoL):** [solonline.org](https://www.solonline.org/)
- **Presencing Institute:** [presencinginstitute.org](https://www.presencing.org/)

## Adjacent publications

### The Systems Thinker

**Live URL:** [https://thesystemsthinker.com/](https://thesystemsthinker.com/) — free online publication, published by Leverage Networks, Inc. (originally Pegasus Communications).

Features articles from the broader MIT system-dynamics / organizational-learning tradition:
- Peter Senge (multiple)
- Donella Meadows (archived)
- Russell Ackoff
- Barry Richmond
- Others

Topics organized by: Archetypes, Fundamentals, Leadership, and by sector (Business, Education, Government, Healthcare, Social Change).

**Not Meadows's own publication**, but the closest thing to a live venue where Meadows-adjacent work continues to be published.

### System Dynamics Society

Live URL: [systemdynamics.org](https://systemdynamics.org/) — the professional society for the discipline Meadows worked in. Publishes the *System Dynamics Review*. Endorses *Thinking in Systems* on its book pages.

### Integration and Implementation Insights (i2insights.org)

Live URL: [i2insights.org](https://i2insights.org/) — practitioner-facing publication in complex-problem research. Frequently references Meadows. E.g., [Key systems thinking lessons from Donella Meadows](https://i2insights.org/2023/10/03/meadows-systems-thinking-lessons/).
