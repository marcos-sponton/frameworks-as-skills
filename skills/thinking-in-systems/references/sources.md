# Thinking in Systems — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says. Because Meadows died in 2001, this is largely an archival index plus the organizational continuation plus modern descendants.

## Primary text — the posthumous book

- **Thinking in Systems: A Primer** — Donella H. Meadows, edited by Diana Wright. Chelsea Green Publishing, 2008. **Posthumous** — drafted around 1993, edited from her manuscript by Diana Wright at the Sustainability Institute for publication seven years after Meadows's death in 2001. [Publisher page](https://www.chelseagreen.com/product/thinking-in-systems/). [Full text PDF](https://research.fit.edu/media/site-specific/researchfitedu/coast-climate-adaptation-library/climate-communications/psychology-amp-behavior/Meadows-2008.-Thinking-in-Systems.pdf).
- [Wikipedia — Thinking In Systems: A Primer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking_In_Systems:_A_Primer) — publication history and editorial notes.
- [System Dynamics Society — Thinking in Systems](https://systemdynamics.org/product/thinking-in-systems-a-primer/) — the discipline's endorsement.

## The canonical essay (arguably more cited than the book)

- **Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System** — Donella Meadows, Sustainability Institute, 1999. The full-length essay. [donellameadows.org — Leverage Points essay](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/). [PDF via Sustainability Institute](https://donellameadows.org/wp-content/userfiles/Leverage_Points.pdf).
- **Places to Intervene in a System** — condensed version, *Whole Earth Review*, Winter 1997 (issue 91). [PDF](https://designsciencelab.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/PlacesInterveneSystem-Meadows.pdf).

## The humility companion

- **Dancing with Systems** — Donella Meadows, 1998 or 1999. [donellameadows.org — Dancing with Systems](https://donellameadows.org/archives/dancing-with-systems/).

## The founding real-world application

- **The Limits to Growth** — Donella Meadows, Dennis Meadows, Jørgen Randers, William Behrens III. Commissioned by the Club of Rome. Universe Books, 1972. Sold 9 million copies in 28 languages.
- **Beyond the Limits** — Donella Meadows, Dennis Meadows, Jørgen Randers. Chelsea Green, 1992. 20-year update.
- **Limits to Growth: The 30-Year Update** — Donella Meadows, Dennis Meadows, Jørgen Randers. Chelsea Green, 2004. Published three years after Donella's death; contains material she contributed before her death.

## The archive

- **The Donella Meadows Project — donellameadows.org** — [https://donellameadows.org/](https://donellameadows.org/). Comprehensive archive of Meadows's writing, run as a project of the Academy for Systems Change. Includes:
  - **Global Citizen columns** (weekly, 1985–2001, ~800 pieces).
  - **Dear Folks Letters** (personal / community correspondence).
  - **Articles & Papers** (including the Leverage Points essay and Dancing with Systems).
  - **Systems Thinking Resources** — [donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-resources/](https://donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-resources/) — Iceberg Model, Bathtub Theorem, Stock and Flow Diagramming, guided envisioning tools.
  - **Media** — In a World of Systems (Macaulay + Booth Sweeney), lecture recordings.
  - **Book Sale** — [donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-book-sale/](https://donellameadows.org/systems-thinking-book-sale/).
- [Donella Meadows, 1941–2001](https://donellameadows.org/donella-meadows-1941-2001/) — eulogy and biographical overview.

## The organizational continuation

- **Academy for Systems Change** — [https://academyforchange.org/](https://academyforchange.org/) — organizational continuation of the Sustainability Institute Meadows founded in 1996 (later Donella Meadows Institute). Publishes the Systems Fieldbook, hosts Field Stories, runs practitioner programs.

## Adjacent publications

- **The Systems Thinker** — [https://thesystemsthinker.com/](https://thesystemsthinker.com/) — free online publication in the MIT system-dynamics tradition. Features archived Meadows articles alongside Senge, Ackoff, Richmond, and others.
- **System Dynamics Society** — [systemdynamics.org](https://systemdynamics.org/) — the professional society for the discipline. Publishes *System Dynamics Review*.
- **Integration and Implementation Insights (i2insights.org)** — [i2insights.org](https://i2insights.org/) — practitioner-facing publication in complex-problem research. See [Key systems thinking lessons from Donella Meadows](https://i2insights.org/2023/10/03/meadows-systems-thinking-lessons/).

## System archetypes — canonical references

- **The System Archetypes** — William Braun, 2002. [PDF](https://letourneaufedericointa.github.io/rumiantes/LinkedDocuments/Braun%20-%202002%20-%20The%20System%20Archetypes.pdf). Widely used formalized enumeration.
- **The Fifth Discipline** — Peter M. Senge, Doubleday, 1990. Systematized the archetypes for organizational learning; direct descendant of the same MIT system-dynamics tradition Meadows worked in.

## Modern descendants — extensions of Meadows's method

- **Doughnut Economics: Seven Ways to Think Like a 21st-Century Economist** — Kate Raworth, Random House, 2017. Direct extension of Meadows's balancing-loop analysis to global economic policy. [doughnuteconomics.org](https://doughnuteconomics.org/).
- **Planetary Boundaries framework** — Johan Rockström and colleagues, Stockholm Resilience Centre, 2009. Direct methodological descent from *The Limits to Growth*. [stockholmresilience.org](https://www.stockholmresilience.org/).
- **Less Is More: How Degrowth Will Save the World** — Jason Hickel, Penguin, 2020. Political-economic extension.
- **Leverage points for sustainability transformations: nine guiding questions** — [Springer Sustainability Science, 2021](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11625-021-00961-8). Academic extension of the leverage-points framework.

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **Meadows's individual Global Citizen columns** — the archive holds ~800 columns; only the framework of the column is indexed here, not individual columns.
- **Original 1972 The Limits to Growth full text** — the book is cited but not deeply worked through in this skill.
- **World3 simulation model** — the technical system-dynamics model underlying *Limits to Growth* is referenced but not documented.
- **Balaton Group history** — the international sustainability network Meadows co-founded with Dennis Meadows in 1982 is not documented here.
- **Post-2001 Academy for Systems Change Fellows** — the Fellowship has run cohorts for years; specific Fellows and their outputs are not systematically indexed.
- **Meadows's academic publications** beyond *Limits to Growth* and *Thinking in Systems* — she published in academic journals; those are not indexed here.
- **Non-English-language Meadows work** — *Limits to Growth* was translated into 28 languages; reception traditions in other languages are largely undocumented here.
- **Peter Senge / Fifth Discipline** in depth — referenced as adjacent tradition but not systematically summarized.
- **System dynamics academic literature** post-Meadows — the discipline continues at MIT and elsewhere; not indexed here.
- **Specific climate-adjacent policy applications** of Meadows's method — extensive practitioner work exists but is not indexed.
- **Applications by Chris Corrigan, Sonja Blignaut, and others at the Cynefin-Meadows intersection** — light indexing.
