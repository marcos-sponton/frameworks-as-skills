# Shreyas Doshi's PM Frameworks -- Prompts

> Invocation templates for common tasks. Use these to start a session with the right framework loaded.

---

## Task prioritization (LNO)

### "Help me classify my tasks"
> I have a list of tasks for this week. Help me classify each one as Leverage (10x impact), Neutral (1x), or Overhead (<1x) using Shreyas Doshi's LNO framework. Then tell me where I'm spending too much energy and what to change.

### "Am I spending my energy right?"
> Here's what I spent my time on last week: [list]. Apply the LNO framework to audit my time distribution. Am I spending enough on Leverage tasks? Where am I perfectionism-spraying on Overhead?

### "What should I focus on today?"
> I have these 8 things on my plate today: [list]. Using the LNO framework and opportunity cost thinking, help me decide which 2-3 deserve my best effort and which I should just get done.

---

## Risk identification (Pre-mortems)

### "Help me run a pre-mortem"
> We're launching [project] in [timeframe]. Walk me through Shreyas Doshi's pre-mortem framework with the Tigers/Paper Tigers/Elephants classification. Help me identify the top risks and create an action plan with DRIs.

### "What could kill this project?"
> Here's our project: [description]. Assume it's 3 months from now and the project has failed spectacularly. What are the Tigers (real threats), Paper Tigers (perceived but manageable), and Elephants (things nobody's talking about)?

### "Review our pre-mortem output"
> We ran a pre-mortem and identified these risks: [list]. Help me classify them as Tigers, Paper Tigers, or Elephants, prioritize the top 3-5, and create an action plan with specific mitigations and owners.

---

## Communication and alignment (Three Levels)

### "Why does my team keep clashing with leadership?"
> I'm a PM and my exec team keeps being frustrated in our product reviews. Apply Shreyas Doshi's Three Levels framework (Impact/Execution/Optics) to diagnose the mismatch. What level am I probably operating at? What level do they want?

### "Help me prepare for an exec review"
> I'm presenting [product/project] to the C-suite next week. Using the Three Levels framework, help me restructure my presentation to operate at the Impact level rather than drowning them in Execution detail.

### "Is this really an execution problem?"
> My team is struggling with [problem]. Everyone says it's an execution problem. Apply Shreyas Doshi's "execution problems are strategy problems" insight to help me investigate whether the real issue is strategy clarity, team alignment, or incentive structure.

---

## Hiring and team (High Agency / Archetypes)

### "What kind of PM should I hire?"
> My startup is at [stage] with these challenges: [list]. Using Shreyas Doshi's Three Types of Product Leaders (Operator/Craftsperson/Visionary) and the Agency-Talent Matrix, help me decide what archetype and agency level I should be hiring for.

### "Assess this PM candidate"
> Here's what I know about a PM candidate: [description]. Using the Agency-Talent Matrix (Game Changer / Frustrated Genius / Go-Getter) and the Three Types framework, help me assess fit for a role with [level of ambiguity].

### "What kind of PM am I?"
> Here's how I typically work: [description]. Using Shreyas Doshi's Three Types of Product Leaders and the High Agency framework, help me understand my default archetype and assess my agency level. What should I develop?

---

## Problem validation (CPSR / Focusing Illusion)

### "Are we solving the right problem?"
> We're building [product/feature] to solve [problem]. Apply Shreyas Doshi's Focusing Illusion diagnosis and CPSR framework. Help me design a stack-ranking exercise to validate whether this is actually the most important problem for our customers.

### "How do I validate problem importance?"
> I've been talking to customers about [problem] and they all say it's important. But I'm worried about the Focusing Illusion. How do I use the Customer Problems Stack Rank to get a more honest picture?

---

## Decision-making (Opportunity Cost)

### "Help me decide between these options"
> I'm choosing between [option A] and [option B]. Apply Shreyas Doshi's Opportunity Cost vs ROI framework. Don't just tell me if each option is "worth doing" -- help me figure out which one minimizes opportunity cost.

### "We have too many priorities"
> Our team has [N] priorities this quarter. Using LNO + Opportunity Cost thinking, help me identify which ones are truly Leverage and which are Neutral or Overhead masquerading as priorities.

---

## Self-development (Antithesis Principle / Product Sense)

### "How do I develop better product judgment?"
> I want to develop stronger Product Sense. Walk me through Shreyas Doshi's five components (empathy, simulation, strategic thinking, taste, creative execution) and help me assess where I'm strong and where I need to develop.

### "Apply the Antithesis Principle to my situation"
> I've been using this insight to influence my team: [insight]. Apply the Antithesis Principle -- what does this truth mean when pointed inward? What default in my own thinking should I examine?

### "Am I really listening?"
> I think I'm a good listener but I'm not sure. Walk me through Shreyas Doshi's framework on listening from the "4 questions I wish I'd asked sooner" episode. What does genuine listening look like vs preparing responses while others speak?

## Career guidance

### "How do I grow as a PM?"
> I'm a [level] PM at [company type]. Using Shreyas Doshi's frameworks -- the 4 questions, the Three Types, Product Sense, and High Agency -- help me design a development plan for the next 12 months.

### "Why am I so busy but not productive?"
> I feel like I'm always busy but my impact reviews are disappointing. Walk me through Shreyas Doshi's "Why am I so busy?" question and apply the LNO framework to help me diagnose where my energy is going wrong.
