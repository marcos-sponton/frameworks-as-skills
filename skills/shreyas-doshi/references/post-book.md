# Evolution and Refinements

> Shreyas Doshi does not have a published book. His body of work lives in Twitter/X threads (2020--2021 primarily), podcast appearances, and his Substack newsletter (2026+). This file tracks how his frameworks have evolved over time -- from the original Twitter threads through podcast appearances to the more recent Substack essays and Maven course material. This is the differential of this skill: the density that surface-level familiarity with "the LNO framework" doesn't capture.

## Timeline of the body of work

### Phase 1: Twitter thread era (2020--2021)

The foundational frameworks were all published as Twitter threads, most during 2020-2021:

- **Apr 2020**: "Product Management in 1 tweet" -- defines PM traits as "Low ego. Deep care. High agency." The earliest public articulation of high agency as a PM trait.
- **May 2020**: CPSR (Customer Problems Stack Rank) thread -- the Focusing Illusion concept applied to product discovery.
- **Jun 2020**: High Agency thread -- the full framework including Agency-Talent Matrix, Low Agency Bob vs High Agency Alice.
- **Feb 2021**: "Why product management is hard" thread.
- **Jan 2021**: Pre-mortems thread -- Tigers/Paper Tigers/Elephants introduced.
- **Mar 2021**: Three Types of Product Leaders (Operator/Craftsperson/Visionary).
- **Apr 2021**: B2B Product Management Story -- visual story thread on customer problem discovery.
- **Jun 2021**: PM Frameworks mega-thread -- curated index of 22+ framework threads.
- **Jun 2021**: LNO Effectiveness Framework thread.
- **Jun 2021**: Opportunity Cost vs ROI thread.
- **Aug 2021**: Three Levels of Product Work (Impact/Execution/Optics).

**What's notable about this phase**: The frameworks arrived fully formed as individual threads. Each one stands alone. Shreyas didn't build a unified system -- he published independent mental models, each attacking a specific PM failure mode. The Twitter medium forced compression and clarity.

### Phase 2: Podcast amplification (2022--2024)

The frameworks expanded through long-form conversation, adding nuance that threads couldn't contain:

- **Jun 2022**: Lenny's Podcast (Episode 3) -- the defining episode. Pre-mortems, LNO, Three Levels, "execution problems are strategy problems," ROI vs opportunity cost. This became one of Lenny's most-cited episodes and brought Shreyas's frameworks to a much larger audience. The episode added:
  - A two-step tactic for overcoming procrastination on Leverage tasks.
  - The insight that "most execution problems are not really execution problems" -- expanded from the Twitter-sized version to a full diagnostic framework.
  - Practical examples from Stripe on pre-mortem culture.

- **Knowledge Project #175** (Shane Parrish) -- added:
  - The Agency-Talent Matrix in a hiring context ("I would rather hire the go-getter").
  - The distinction between measurement and evaluation.
  - The Antithesis Principle (first discussed here before the 2026 Substack essay).
  - The insight about organizational values: "When the founders are actually living the values that are put up on the wall, that means that they're not just words on a wall."
  - The importance of writing culture: "By forcing ourselves to write down our thinking, it enables the reaching of consensus or a decision better than if we were trying to do it all just in a meeting."

- **20 Product** (Harry Stebbings) -- added:
  - The 6 types of product metrics.
  - Table stakes features vs wow features.
  - Detailed guidance on hiring by archetype based on startup stage.

- **Amplitude blog conversation with John Cutler** -- added:
  - Mindset / Principles / Tactics hierarchy: tactics provide immediate productivity, principles enable flexibility, mindset determines whether you apply either consistently.
  - Self-managing teams: create autonomy through clear principles and structured rituals, not constant oversight.
  - Dashboards as products: treat metrics dashboards as products -- track who views them, reduce friction to increase engagement.
  - The "shapeshifter" identity trap: PMs who identify with handling everything struggle to delegate as leaders.
  - "Don't believe everything I write" -- the explicit self-counter against dogmatic adoption.

- **Oct 2024**: Lenny's Podcast live episode -- "4 questions Shreyas Doshi wishes he'd asked himself sooner":
  1. "Why am I so busy?" -- connecting back to LNO and opportunity cost thinking.
  2. "Do I actually have good taste?" -- connecting to Product Sense.
  3. "Why does my job feel so frustrating?" -- connecting to the Three Levels mismatch.
  4. "Am I really listening?" -- the listening skill, referencing Rick Rubin, Dee Hock, and Peter Drucker.

**What's notable about this phase**: The podcasts didn't replace the threads -- they added depth. Shreyas tells stories, provides examples, and connects frameworks to each other in ways the threads couldn't. The Lenny's episode (Jun 2022) is the single most important piece in his catalog because it reaches the largest audience and contains the highest density of connected frameworks.

### Phase 3: Substack essay era (2026+)

Shreyas launched his Substack newsletter ("Shreyas Free Newsletter") as a primary channel for new thinking. The essays are more reflective, philosophical, and less framework-oriented than the Twitter threads. They extend and deepen rather than replace the earlier frameworks.

Key essays and what they add:

- **"The Antithesis Principle"** (Jul 2026) -- Formalized a concept first discussed on the Knowledge Project. Not a PM framework per se, but a meta-framework for how to use any insight: point it inward first.

- **"The Fundamental Cognitive Bias of Product Builders"** (Jul 2026) -- Extends the Focusing Illusion beyond customer discovery into the builders' own thinking patterns.

- **"Do you know what you are really selling?"** (Jun 2026) -- Deepens the CPSR insight: the problem you're solving is not always what you think you're selling.

- **"On Blunt Feedback"** (May 2026) -- Practical guidance on giving feedback, connecting to the Three Levels (feedback at the wrong level is counterproductive).

- **"Get to the Core of the Thing"** (May 2026) -- A diagnostic discipline that cuts across all frameworks: don't address symptoms.

- **"9 axioms of Interpersonal Communication"** (Mar 2026) -- Extends the Three Levels concept into interpersonal dynamics beyond product reviews.

- **"Why Product Sense is the only product skill that will matter in the AI age"** (Mar 2026) -- The most significant evolution. Defines Product Sense precisely (five components: empathy, simulation, strategic thinking, taste, creative execution) and argues it's the only sustainable career moat. Key claim: "Tools have never been a significant source of alpha in product success and that is not changing with AI tools."

- **"The Humility Trap"** (Feb 2026) -- Challenges performative humility. "Chasing humility will surely keep you busy, but it is pointless." Three levels of authentic humility: learning from those you don't consider superior, sharing what you learned from others, recognizing that pursuing humility itself perpetuates status games.

- **"Route Around Big Egos"** (Jan 2026) -- Practical advice for navigating combative PM dynamics. Route negotiations through EMs/TLs when PM egos block alignment. "The smarter the person, the better the excuse."

- **"Influence, Power, and Product Management in Modern Product Companies"** (Jan 2026) -- Examines the structural tension of PM influence.

### Phase 4: Maven course (ongoing)

The Maven course "World-class Product Sense in Practice" represents the most structured and operational version of Shreyas's thinking. 600+ alumni, $2,700, 8-day intensive.

What the course adds beyond public material:
- **20 creativity techniques** for solving product problems.
- **Detailed positioning and segmentation frameworks** -- moving beyond traditional SMB/enterprise categorizations.
- **Taste development as a discipline** -- not just "have good taste" but specific practices for building and communicating aesthetic judgment.
- **16+ hours of private material** on influence, leadership, and advising.

The course is where Product Sense gets its most precise articulation. The Substack essay (Mar 2026) is the public-facing version; the course is the operational one.

## Key evolutionary threads

### From separate frameworks to interconnected worldview

The 2020-2021 threads were independent. Over time, Shreyas has increasingly connected them:
- LNO + Opportunity Cost: Both attack the same problem (misallocated energy) from different angles.
- CPSR + Focusing Illusion + Product Sense: The chain from problem validation to decision quality to judgment as a moat.
- Three Levels + "Execution problems are strategy problems": Both diagnose communication and alignment failures.
- High Agency + Antithesis Principle: Both are about self-awareness and self-development, not just outward tactics.

### From PM-specific to leadership-general

The early threads were explicitly PM frameworks. The Substack essays (Antithesis Principle, Humility Trap, Blunt Feedback, Interpersonal Communication) apply to any knowledge worker or leader. Shreyas's audience has expanded beyond PMs to founders, designers, engineers, and executives.

### From tactical to philosophical

The LNO thread is pure tactics: label your tasks, allocate energy. The Antithesis Principle is philosophy: examine how you use truth itself. The trajectory is from "here's a framework for your to-do list" to "here's a framework for how you think about thinking."

### Product Sense as the integrating concept

If there's one concept that increasingly sits above all others in Shreyas's worldview, it's Product Sense. In the AI age essay (Mar 2026), he argues that Product Sense -- the combination of empathy, simulation, strategic thinking, taste, and creative execution -- is the only durable career moat. This implicitly positions all his other frameworks as tools that develop Product Sense components: CPSR develops empathy, LNO develops strategic thinking, the Antithesis Principle develops self-awareness that feeds taste.
