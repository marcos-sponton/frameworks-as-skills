# Never Split the Difference — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts

- **Never Split the Difference: Negotiating As If Your Life Depended On It** — Christopher Voss with Tahl Raz. HarperBusiness, 2016. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Never-Split-Difference-Negotiating-Depended/dp/0062407805). The canonical source for tactical empathy, the nine tools, Ackerman, Black Swans.
- **The Full Fee Agent: How to Stack the Odds in Your Favor as a Real Estate Professional** — Chris Voss & Steve Shull. 2022. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Full-Fee-Agent-Estate-Professional/dp/154454085X). Applied to real-estate fee negotiation; generalizes to any professional-services fee-holding situation.

## Adjacent Voss / Black Swan books

- **Ego, Authority, Failure: Using Emotional Intelligence Like a Hostage Negotiator to Succeed as a Leader** — Derek Gaunt (Black Swan Group instructor). 2019. Not authored by Voss but applies the Black Swan Method to leadership. Adjacent, method-carrying.

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **Full read-through of *Never Split the Difference* (2016) or *The Full Fee Agent* (2022)** — worked from the books' publicly-available material (excerpts, HBR-adjacent articles, MasterClass, podcast transcripts, dense summaries). Book-only detail may be missing.
- **Full Lex Fridman #364 transcript line-by-line** — cited from summaries and known extract.
- **Full MasterClass — 18 chapters, each in detail** — worked from published chapter overviews.
- **Individual Black Swan Group training materials** — proprietary; not publicly available.
- **Derek Gaunt's *Ego, Authority, Failure* (2019)** — noted as adjacent but not read for this skill.
- **Recent podcast appearances 2025+** — not yet catalogued.
- **Brandon Voss / Steve Shull / Sandy Hein individual bylines** — worth tracking for readers going deep.
- **HBS / Georgetown / USC adjunct teaching materials** — mostly private.
- **Community critiques and misapplications in the wild** — practitioners applying the method often surface gotchas Voss doesn't. (In particular, the weaponization risk is under-documented in Voss's own writing but heavily discussed by third-party critics.)
- **Cross-cultural adaptation studies** — the extreme anchor's cultural variability is noted but not systematically documented.
