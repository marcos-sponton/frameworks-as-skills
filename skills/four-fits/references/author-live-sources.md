# Four Fits — Brian Balfour's live sources

> **Live sources** = places where Brian Balfour publishes new material regularly. This file is the index into all of them, organized by source type and by topic. It's designed for the skill to jump to the specific essay/video/podcast that matches the user's situation.
>
> **Why it matters:** Four Fits is not frozen in 2017. The 2018 growth-loops reframing, the 2024–2025 AI-era update, and the ongoing essay cadence all reshape how the framework applies. Skipping live sources = the skill drifts out of date. Community PRs to this file are the fastest way to keep the skill current.
>
> **How to use in a session:** when the user has a specific situation (Danger Zone diagnosis, ChatGPT-as-channel, growth-team hiring, freemium under AI cost pressure), scan this index for the matching item and either (a) suggest they read/listen, or (b) WebFetch it inline if depth is needed.

## Primary essay source — brianbalfour.com

**Live URL:** [https://brianbalfour.com/essays](https://brianbalfour.com/essays)

Balfour's personal essay archive. Free, public. The original 2017 Four Fits series lives here. New essays land irregularly (~a few per year) with high signal density.

### Four Fits — the canonical essays

- [Four Fits For $100M+ Growth](https://brianbalfour.com/four-fits-growth-framework) — the 2017 anchor essay.
- [Why Product Market Fit Isn't Enough](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/product-market-fit-isnt-enough) — the framing argument.
- [The Model Market Fit Threshold & What it Means for Your Growth Strategy](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/model-market-fit-threshold-for-growth) — the `ARPU × TAM × %` formula + the five archetypes.
- [Get Out of the ARPU-CAC Danger Zone with Channel Model Fit](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/channel-model-fit-for-user-acquisition) — the five ARPU zones.
- [Building a Growth Framework Towards a $100 Million Product](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/hubspot-growth-framework-100m) — HubSpot as case.
- [Why Most Companies Fail At Moving Up or Down Market](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/key-lessons-for-100m-growth) — Danger Zone escape moves.

### Growth Loops

- [Growth Loops are the New Funnels](https://www.reforge.com/blog/growth-loops) — Reforge blog, 2018. Co-authored with Casey Winters, Kevin Kwok, Andrew Chen.
- [The Universal Growth Loop](https://brianbalfour.com/quick-takes/universal-growth-loop) — company-level flywheel.
- [A Breakdown of LinkedIn's AI-Assisted Growth Loop](https://brianbalfour.com/essays/a-breakdown-of-linkedins-ai-assisted-growth-loop) — 2024–2025 case study.

### Growth machine / team / operating layer
