# The Lean Startup — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts

- **The Lean Startup: How Today's Entrepreneurs Use Continuous Innovation to Create Radically Successful Businesses** — Eric Ries. Crown Business, 2011. [Amazon 0307887898](https://www.amazon.com/Lean-Startup-Entrepreneurs-Continuous-Innovation/dp/0307887898). New York Times bestseller. The canonical text — read it.

- **The Leader's Guide** — Eric Ries. Kickstarter, 2016. Kickstarter-only, distributed to backers. Not commercially reissued. Historical bridge between *The Lean Startup* and *The Startup Way*.

- **The Startup Way: How Modern Companies Use Entrepreneurial Management to Transform Culture and Drive Long-Term Growth** — Eric Ries. Currency, 2017. [Amazon 1101903201](https://www.amazon.com/Startup-Way-Companies-Entrepreneurial-Management/dp/1101903201). The enterprise extension — GE, Toyota, Intuit, Pitney Bowes; Growth Board governance; entrepreneur-as-org-chart-role.

- **Incorruptible: Why Good Companies Go Bad… and How Great Companies Stay Great** — Eric Ries. Authors Equity, May 26, 2026. [Amazon B0FWZZBPZB](https://www.amazon.com/Incorruptible-Good-Companies-Great-Stay/dp/B0FWZZBPZB) · [Simon & Schuster](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Incorruptible/Eric-Ries/9798893311860). New York Times bestseller. The governance frame — financial gravity, spiritual holding company, governance-as-product-design.

## Legacy blog posts (startuplessonslearned.com, 2008–2015)

Historical primary sources. Still-live URLs.

- [Five Whys](http://www.startuplessonslearned.com/2008/11/five-whys.html) — November 2008. Origin of Ries's Five Whys writeup. Contains the canonical CPU-spike example and "Behind every seemingly technical problem is actually a human problem waiting to be found."

## Guest posts

- [Vanity Metrics vs. Actionable Metrics](https://tim.blog/2009/05/19/vanity-metrics-vs-actionable-metrics/) — Ries guest post on tim.blog, May 2009. Origin of Ries's vanity-vs-actionable framing. Contains the four preferred devices (split-tests, per-customer metrics, funnel/cohort analysis, keyword metrics).
