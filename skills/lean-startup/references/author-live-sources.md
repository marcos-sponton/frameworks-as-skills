## Primary live channels (current, active as of mid-2026)

### The Eric Ries Show — podcast
- **Landing:** https://ltse.com/the-eric-ries-show
- **Apple Podcasts:** https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-eric-ries-show/id1744818044
- **Spotify:** https://open.spotify.com/show/1PA861kDcuviHDqTi2AmuC
- **Cadence:** bi-weekly-ish; irregular but active through 2024–2026. No fixed weekly schedule.
- **Format:** long-form (60–120 min) conversations with technologists, operators, and thinkers on **growth with purpose, long-term company building, mission-controlled organizations.**
- **What to reach for it for:** current-Ries voice on any topic; his 2024–2026 thinking; guest cases from the mission-controlled operating world.

### Newsletter — news.theleanstartup.com
- **URL:** https://news.theleanstartup.com/
- **Cadence:** irregular; heavy during launch cycles (e.g., May 2026 Incorruptible launch had multiple posts per week).
- **Tagline:** *"why great companies go bad — and what founders, CEOs, and operators can do about it."*
- **Recent 2026 posts (Incorruptible cycle):** "Corruption, Defined" · "What is profit?" · "Harder is Easier. Here's Why."
- **What to reach for it for:** Ries's own words on the current-book-cycle theme; sharp definitional essays.

### leanstartup.co — Lean Startup Company (training + conference)
- **URL:** https://leanstartup.co
- **What to reach for it for:** curated articles including [Ries on 4 Common Misconceptions](https://leanstartup.co/resources/articles/eric-ries-on-4-common-misconceptions-about-lean-startup/) and the [FastWorks Retrospective](https://leanstartup.co/resources/articles/fastworks-reflecting-origin-evolution/). ### startuplessonslearned.com — the origin blog (2008–2015, dormant)
- **URL:** http://www.startuplessonslearned.com/
- **Historical importance:** where Five Whys, MVP, Innovation Accounting, and vanity vs. actionable metrics were first published. **The 2008 Five Whys post** (http://www.startuplessonslearned.com/2008/11/five-whys.html) is still the canonical source for that device.
- **What to reach for it for:** primary-source citation on the pre-book devices. If a user says "where did Ries first define X?", this is often the answer.
