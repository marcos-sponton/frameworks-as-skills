## Primary channels

### The Re-Wired Group — therewiredgroup.com

Detroit-based "innovation incubator" / consultancy. Moesta is President & CEO; Greg Engle is co-founder.

- **Positioning:** "Flip the lens on your innovation practice."
- **Content types:** articles, playbooks, guides, case studies.
- **Programs:** 10-week coaching programs, JTBD workshops, custom client engagements.
- **Clients (public):** Hudl, Intercom, Target, SNHU, AutoBooks.
- **Signature language:** "customer blind spots", "the messy middle", "why your customers hire you."
- **URL:** [therewiredgroup.com](https://therewiredgroup.com/)

### jobstobedone.org — the JTBD method library

Run by Moesta + **Chris Spiek** (co-inventor of the Forces diagram). The most comprehensive free JTBD resource.

- **57+ articles** on the method — theory, application, anti-patterns.
- **15+ recorded real Switch Interviews** — the mattress interview is the most famous, used in Christensen's *Competing Against Luck*.
- **JTBD Radio podcast** — guests include Christensen, Jason Fried, Des Traynor.
- **Paid course:** "Mastering JTBD Interviews" ($400 lifetime access).
- **URL:** [jobstobedone.org](https://jobstobedone.org/)

**Best for:** when the user wants to hear an actual Switch Interview, learn the interview technique from examples, or go deep on the Forces.

### The Circuit Breaker podcast

Hosted by Bob Moesta + Greg Engle. This is Bob's "own" show — more intimate and methodological than his guest appearances.

- **Episode types:** unusually personal and meta — e.g., "The Secret Language of Bob-isms" unpacks Moesta's own catchphrases. Other topics: "Unpacking Innovation", "Listening", "Learning to Build", "Gut Check."
- **Cadence:** ongoing, irregular.
- **URL:** [therewiredgroup.com/circuit-breaker-podcast/](https://therewiredgroup.com/circuit-breaker-podcast/)
- **Also on:** Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, Podbay, Listen Notes.

**Best for:** when the user wants Moesta's unfiltered thinking on a specific topic, or wants to understand the meta-method behind the method.
