# Demand-Side Sales — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts

- **Demand-Side Sales 101: Stop Selling and Help Your Customers Make Progress** — Bob Moesta & Greg Engle. Lioncrest, 2020. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Demand-Side-Sales-101-Customers-Progress/dp/1544509987). The canonical demand-side sales method. Read it.

- **Learning to Build: The 5 Bedrock Skills of Innovators and Entrepreneurs** — Bob Moesta. Lioncrest, 2022. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Learning-Build-Bedrock-Innovators-Entrepreneurs/dp/1544524005). The innovation lens — five bedrock skills, Deming/Taguchi lineage, Moesta's origin story.

- **Job Moves: 9 Steps for Making Progress in Your Career** — Bob Moesta, Ethan Bernstein & Michael Horn. HarperCollins, Nov 2024. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Job-Moves-Steps-Making-Progress/dp/0063280477) · [jobmoves.com](https://www.jobmoves.com/). The career lens — Four Quests, energy audit, features vs. experiences.

- **Competing Against Luck: The Story of Innovation and Customer Choice** — Clayton Christensen, Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon & David Duncan (Bob Moesta acknowledged). HarperBusiness, 2016. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Competing-Against-Luck-Innovation-Customer/dp/0062435612). The JTBD theory book.

- **Choosing College: How to Make Better Learning Decisions Throughout Your Life** — Michael Horn & Bob Moesta. Jossey-Bass, 2019. [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Choosing-College-Making-Better-Learning/dp/1119570115). JTBD applied to higher-ed.

## What this skill did NOT consult (gaps)

Being explicit so users can PR to close them.

- **The full text of *Learning to Build* (2022)** — only public summaries, SlideShare presentations, and podcast coverage. The five bedrock skills description is reconstructed, not quoted from the book.
- **The full text of *Job Moves* (2024)** — the nine steps are reconstructed from public coverage, not the book's exact structure. Corrections from the actual text are especially valuable.
- **Chris Spiek's Substack** — Spiek publishes JTBD interview recordings; these are not individually indexed here.
- **Re-Wired Group's paid course "Mastering JTBD Interviews"** — referenced but not consulted.
- **Bob Moesta's LinkedIn posts 2025-2026** — auth-walled; cadence and content patterns described from prior observation, not live scraping.
- **The Circuit Breaker podcast's full episode archive** — only the "Bob-isms" episode and general description are indexed. The full archive likely contains additional method refinements.
- **Individual BoS Conference recordings beyond the indexed talks** — Moesta has given 3+ BoS talks; only the most cited are indexed.
