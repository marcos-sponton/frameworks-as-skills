## Explicit gaps

Documented so future contributors know where to sharpen:

- **No dedicated YouTube channel.** Video content is scattered on institutional channels.
- **No Substack, Medium, or personal blog** for either author. All post-book essay-length material lives on the firm's blog.
- **No AI-era commentary** from either author at essay length. The mechanisms are portrayed as technology-neutral in principle; any application to AI-native products in this skill is inference rather than direct authorial position.
- **Sparse non-Amazon cases from the authors themselves.** The firm has begun applying the framework externally (Netflix / Warner Bros. blog post) but has not published a book's worth of external case studies. Cases beyond Kindle / Prime / AWS / Fire Phone / Alexa / Prime Video / Amazon Music are usually secondary applications rather than authorial cases.
- **The AWS repo of the WBR App is public** but I did not code-review it during dossier compilation; if a user needs to understand WBR mechanics at the software level, that repo is the authoritative next step.
- **Colin Bryar solo appearances are thinner than Bill Carr solo appearances** in the sources I could surface. Joint interviews are more common. If the user needs Bryar-specific material, First Round Review and joint firm content are the best entry points.
