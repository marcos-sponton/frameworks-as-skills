# Working Backwards — Applications

> When the method fits, when it doesn't, and what to reach for instead.

## Situations where Working Backwards fits well

- **Deciding whether to build a new product or feature.** The PR/FAQ is designed exactly for this — it forces articulation of customer need before commitment of resources.
- **Critiquing an existing product doc, PRD, or spec.** The Press Release + Internal FAQ shape gives you five diagnostic lenses (customer, problem, solution, adoption, TAM).
- **Structuring a product-review meeting that's currently a PowerPoint exercise.** The 6-pager + silent reading protocol is the direct fix.
- **Setting up input metrics for a team that's currently tracking outputs.** Input Metrics thinking decomposes revenue/growth into controllable levers.
- **Structuring a team around a new initiative — especially one currently stuck in a matrix.** STL is the mechanism.
- **Designing a hiring bar for a critical role, or fixing a hiring process that keeps lowering the bar under urgency.** Bar Raiser is the mechanism.
- **Post-mortem after a real failure.** COE is the structured mechanism.
- **Setting up an operating cadence for a company that's scaling past its founder-instinct phase.** WBR is the mechanism.
- **Post-Series-C or public-company scale-ups importing "how do we operate like Amazon."** The firm's explicit ICP.
- **Argumenting *against* a compensation design that has hard-coded bonuses to plan targets.** The 2026 "doom loop" post is the reference.
- **Building an innovation initiative inside a mature company.** The "separate innovation from core" 2026 framing.

## Situations where it does NOT fit

- **Pre-product/market-fit search.** A full PR/FAQ is overkill when the cost of a wrong bet is a two-week Lean experiment. Working Backwards LLC explicitly targets Series C+ and public companies. Pre-PMF, use **Lean Startup**, **Continuous Discovery** (Torres), or the discovery/validation loops native to your stage.

  Redirect the user honestly: "you're in a search phase; PR/FAQ is a commitment-phase mechanism. Try a lighter shape first — customer interviews, MVP, prototype — and come back to Working Backwards when the cost per bet is high enough to justify weeks on a document."

- **Purely corporate-strategy questions at the "how will we win in this market" altitude.** That's **Playing to Win** (Martin) or **Rumelt's kernel** territory. Working Backwards operates at the product/initiative level, not the corporate-strategy level. A company can (and often should) use Playing to Win at the corporate level and Working Backwards at the product level, but don't run one when the user needs the other.

- **Industry-structure questions.** "Should we be in this industry at all?" is **Porter's Five Forces** or **Wardley Mapping** territory. Working Backwards presupposes you're playing in a market; it doesn't help you choose the market.

- **Portfolio prioritization across N candidate bets.** Working Backwards makes each bet honest through the PR/FAQ, but it doesn't rank across a portfolio. For portfolio thinking, look at **ODI (Ulwick)**, **JTBD priority scoring**, or **RICE**.

- **Culture, hiring, or org-design questions without a product-decision angle.** Bar Raiser is the WB mechanism for hiring, but if the user's question is "how do we build a great culture", they need something upstream of any mechanism — probably **Amazon's Leadership Principles** as substrate, or a broader values/culture framework.

- **Crisis or turnaround where the diagnosis is more urgent than the mechanism.** Use **Rumelt's diagnosis-first approach** to find the crux, then bring in Working Backwards mechanisms for the specific bets you'll make in response.

- **Fast-moving early-stage teams where the review overhead would kill velocity.** Working Backwards mechanisms add friction on purpose. That friction is valuable when the cost per bet is high. For a 3-person team shipping daily, the friction just kills you.

- **When the user just wants a summary of the book.** Give them the book link. Don't run the mechanism at them.

## Adjacent frameworks — when to reach for a different one

| If the user's situation is... | Reach for... | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-PMF startup search | **Lean Startup** (Ries), **Continuous Discovery** (Torres) | Search, not commitment |
| Corporate strategy at "how will we win" altitude | **Playing to Win** (Martin) or **Rumelt's kernel** | Higher altitude than Working Backwards |
| Diagnosis of what's broken before choosing | **Rumelt's kernel** (diagnosis / guiding policy / coherent action) | Rumelt front-loads diagnosis |
| Understanding industry structure | **Porter's Five Forces** | Industry-level, not product-level |
| Mapping evolutionary landscape | **Wardley Mapping** | Positioning over time |
| Ranking N candidate bets | **ODI (Ulwick)**, **JTBD priority scoring**, **RICE** | Working Backwards makes each bet honest, doesn't rank across |
| Deep customer-need research feeding the "problem paragraph" | **JTBD (Moesta / Ulwick / Christensen)**, **Design Thinking**, **Continuous Discovery** | Complementary — feeds the PR/FAQ input |
| Fast product iteration on a shipped product | **Continuous Discovery**, **Dual-Track Agile** | WB is heavier; use lighter loops for shipped products |
| Building the enabling org culture | **Amazon's 14 Leadership Principles** (as substrate), or your own equivalent | Mechanisms depend on Principles |
| Setting up individual/team OKRs | Working Backwards is *against* OKRs-as-strategy. Use input metrics + WBR + long-term equity comp instead. | See the compensation-planning doom loop essay |

## Common misapplications to redirect

### The user wants "an Amazon-style memo template"

If there's no customer problem yet to work through, redirect. Templates are downstream of the actual customer question. Start with: who is your customer, what problem do they have, why does it matter to them? *Then* the template shape makes sense.

### The user wants Working Backwards for a pre-PMF startup

Redirect gently. Full PR/FAQ is overkill when a two-week experiment can validate the same hypothesis. Suggest Lean Startup or Continuous Discovery instead, and note that Working Backwards becomes valuable when they hit Series C+ scale.

### The user wants to adopt PR/FAQ but skip silent reading

Push back. The silent-reading meeting is what makes the PR/FAQ interrogatable. Skip it and you have a document no one has actually read carefully — the mechanism collapses.

### The user names an "STL" who's fractional

Push back. A "leader" split across three initiatives is the pre-STL state Amazon rejected. Either dedicate the person fully, or don't call it STL.

### The user is thinking about Working Backwards as corporate strategy

Redirect to Playing to Win or Rumelt. Working Backwards operates at the product/initiative level. Corporate strategy questions ("what markets do we compete in", "how do we differentiate at the company level") need a strategy framework, not a product framework.

### The user asks "does Working Backwards apply to AI-native products?"

Note the gap honestly: Carr and Bryar haven't spoken to this directly. The mechanisms are technology-neutral in principle (a PR/FAQ works whether the product uses LLMs or not; input metrics decompose whether the underlying tech is AI or not), but any specific application to AI-native products is your extrapolation, not the authors' position. Treat as informed inference.

### The user asks Working Backwards to solve a culture problem

Reframe. Working Backwards produces mechanisms; culture is downstream of mechanisms. The right question isn't "how do we adopt Working Backwards to fix our culture" — it's "which of our current mechanisms are producing the culture we don't want, and what mechanism would produce different behavior?" That's a longer conversation than a PR/FAQ workshop.

## Fit criteria in one sentence

Working Backwards fits when: (a) the cost per bet is high enough to justify weeks or months of upfront thinking, (b) there is a real customer whose problem you can name specifically, (c) you have CEO-level air cover to hold the mechanism disposition (silent reading, most PR/FAQs rejected, non-matrixed STLs, Bar Raisers with veto), and (d) you're willing to be told your current bet is wrong.

If any of those four is absent, the mechanism will fight the environment and usually lose. Name the missing condition to the user; that's more honest than shipping mechanism theater.
