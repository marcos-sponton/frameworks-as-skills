# DORA / Accelerate — Sources

> Every source consulted while building this skill, with links. Users should be able to verify anything the skill says.

## Primary texts

- **[*Accelerate: The Science of Lean Software and DevOps: Building and Scaling High Performing Technology Organizations*](https://itrevolution.com/product/accelerate/)** — Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim. IT Revolution Press, 2018. Shingo Research and Professional Publication Award, 2019. The canonical source. Read it.

- **[*Frictionless: 7 Steps to Remove Barriers, Unlock Value, and Outpace Your Competition in the AI Era*](https://www.amazon.com/Frictionless-Remove-Barriers-Outpace-Competition/dp/1662966377)** — Nicole Forsgren, Abi Noda. December 2025. The most recent book — focused on the AI era.

- **[*The DevOps Handbook*, 2nd Edition](https://itrevolution.com/product/the-devops-handbook-second-edition/)** — Gene Kim, Jez Humble, Patrick Debois, John Willis, Nicole Forsgren. IT Revolution Press, 2021.

## Academic papers

- **[The SPACE of Developer Productivity](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3454124)** — Nicole Forsgren, Margaret-Anne Storey, Chandra Maddila, Thomas Zimmermann, Brian Houck, Jenna Butler. ACM Queue, 2021. Also published in Communications of the ACM.

- **[DevEx: What Actually Drives Productivity](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3595878)** — Abi Noda, Margaret-Anne Storey, Nicole Forsgren, Michaela Greiler. ACM Queue, 2023. Also: [Communications of the ACM](https://cacm.acm.org/practice/devex-what-actually-drives-productivity/).

- **[Nicole Forsgren on Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vis0ZxUAAAAJ&hl=en)** — full academic publication list.

- **Full ACM Queue papers behind paywall** — SPACE and DevEx papers themselves were unavailable (403). Content reconstructed from DX summaries, Pragmatic Engineer coverage, and secondary sources.
- **Full transcript of Lenny 2025 episode** — only episode description and secondary coverage consulted.
- **State of DevOps 2020 report** — coverage skipped due to COVID-related data-collection challenges that year.
- **Deep read of the 2019, 2021, 2022 State of DevOps reports** — key findings extracted from secondary summaries.
- **Forsgren's DevOps Enterprise Summit talks** — indexed in `author-live-sources.md` but not transcribed.
- **A broader roster of specific enterprise case studies** — DORA research is largely survey-based and anonymized; the public case library is smaller than for author-centric frameworks.
- **Detailed treatment of the SPACE X extension paper** — arXiv 2025 paper referenced in search results, not consulted.
- **Jez Humble and Gene Kim as co-authors** — their individual public work would enrich the skill; only referenced through *Accelerate* here.
- **Abi Noda's independent DX work** — Frictionless and DX Core 4 are joint; Noda's individual public work is under-consulted.
